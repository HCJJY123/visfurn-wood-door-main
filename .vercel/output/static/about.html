Quick Answer

What does VISFURN supply?

VISFURN focuses on B2B door supply for international procurement. The product scope includes interior, wooden, WPC, entrance, and steel security doors, with specialized project solutions for hotels, schools, and healthcare facilities. We provide specification support for dimensions, wall thickness, frames, hardware, and export packing.
