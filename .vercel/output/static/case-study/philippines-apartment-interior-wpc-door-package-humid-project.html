Direct Answer

For Philippines apartment projects, a mixed package should separate dry-room interior doors from WPC bathroom doors, then control frame depth, finish approval, hardware preparation and packing sequence by room type.

Representative project brief

A Philippines apartment package often combines repeated interior bedroom doors with WPC bathroom doors. The procurement challenge is not only choosing the door style, but keeping sizes, frames, finishes, hardware and packing sequence clear across many identical units.

Why WPC bathroom doors were separated from interior doors

Bathrooms and utility rooms face higher humidity than bedrooms and corridors. Separating WPC bathroom doors in the schedule helps buyers control material choice, frame details and packing labels without confusing installers on site.

Schedule and finish review before production

  • Confirm door leaf size and rough opening by room type.
  • Approve finish boards for interior wooden doors.
  • Confirm WPC color and frame detail for bathroom doors.
  • Match hinge, lock and handle preparation before mass production.
  • Group packing by building, floor, unit or room type.

Opening-control details for similar apartment packages

For repeated units, the schedule is easier to review when each door type keeps one opening ID, room type, finished wall thickness, frame configuration, finish direction, handing, hardware preparation and packing sequence. Bathroom WPC sets should remain distinct from bedroom and corridor door sets even when they ship in the same project package.

  • Use a plan or handing chart for each distinct door type.
  • Record finished wall thickness where frames meet tiled wet-area walls.
  • Separate WPC bathroom openings from dry interior openings in the schedule.
  • Keep packing labels aligned with the floor, unit and opening ID.
