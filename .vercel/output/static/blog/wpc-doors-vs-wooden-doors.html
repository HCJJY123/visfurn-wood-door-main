Door Buying Guide

WPC doors vs wooden doors: where each works best

Direct Answer

WPC doors are usually selected for humid rooms, bathrooms and budget-sensitive projects. Wooden and engineered wooden doors are commonly selected for bedrooms, apartments, hotels and distributor catalogs that need a warmer premium appearance.

Key takeaways

  • Choose WPC doors when moisture resistance and cost control are the priority.
  • Choose wooden doors when appearance, texture and premium room presentation matter more.
  • For hotel projects, confirm acoustic, fire-rated and surface durability requirements early.
  • For distributor orders, compare sample approval, color stability and packing method.
  • Always confirm frame, hardware and finish together with the door leaf.

Engineered for Extreme Environments

1. Thermal Stability (45°C+ Heat Resistance)

Standard timber doors often warp or crack in extreme desert climates. VISFURN WPC doors use a high-density composite structure that remains physically stable even in constant temperatures exceeding 45°C (113°F). Our material formula is specifically tested to prevent expansion and contraction in arid environments like the Middle East.

2. 100% Humidity Barrier

In tropical regions with humidity consistently above 90%, traditional wooden frames can absorb moisture, leading to rot and mold. The WPC (Wood-Plastic Composite) cell structure acts as a total moisture barrier, making it the preferred choice for bathroom, laundry, and coastal project applications.
