Door Hardware Preparation Checklist for Bulk Wooden Door Orders

What to confirm before lock, hinge and closer preparation is released for a bulk door order.

Buying guideHardware coordination

Quick answer

For a bulk wooden door quotation, send the door mark, quantity, handing, opening direction, leaf and frame sizes, wall thickness, hinge and lock preparation, closer requirement, finish, hardware schedule and destination. Hardware-specific machining should follow approved templates rather than assumptions.

Start with the door mark and opening direction

Every hardware decision should be traceable to a door mark in the schedule. Confirm left or right handing, push or pull direction, single or pair door, active leaf, inactive leaf, frame side and any access-control requirement. This prevents a correct-looking hardware set from being installed on the wrong opening.

Schedule fieldWhat to confirmWhy it matters
Door mark and quantityRoom or opening reference, quantity and repeated typeKeeps machining, packing and installation records aligned

Separate hardware selection from hardware preparation

A buyer may select a lever handle or lock family before the final machining template is available. Treat these as two separate checkpoints: first approve the hardware set, then issue the exact drilling, mortise and hinge preparation data. This distinction is especially important for hotel rooms, apartment entry doors and access-controlled openings.

  • Confirm the lock body dimensions and backset.
  • Confirm hinge size, quantity and screw position.
  • Confirm closer, drop seal, flush bolt and strike requirements.
  • Confirm whether hardware is factory-supplied or buyer-supplied.

Freeze the revision before production

Before deposit or production release, keep one approved revision of the door schedule, finish board and hardware preparation sheet. If a hotel operator, contractor or distributor changes the handle or lock after approval, record the revision date and affected door marks so the supplier can confirm cost and lead-time impact.

For a quotation review, send the door schedule, hardware list, frame details, finish references, quantity and destination port together rather than as separate messages.
