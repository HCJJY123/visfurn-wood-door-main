French Doors and Glazed Double Doors: Vision Glass, Security and Hardware Specification

How to specify glass type, muntin layout, security film and matching hardware for French doors and glazed double-door sets.

Glazed Door GuideDouble-door hardware

Quick answer

Specify French and glazed double doors by confirming the glass safety requirement, muntin layout, leaf and frame construction, active and inactive leaf, meeting detail, lock set and any closer or coordinator hardware before the door set is released.

French doors are a full door-set specification

French doors and glazed double doors are not only a leaf style. The project must identify the glazing, grid or muntin construction, meeting stiles, active leaf, locking arrangement, hinges, closer or sequencer hardware and the frame condition. These details affect both appearance and daily operation.

Glass and grid choices

DetailWhat to defineWhy it matters
Glass typeSafety, privacy, acoustic or security requirementThe selected glass must suit the intended opening and use
Muntin layoutTrue divided construction, applied grid or approved visual patternGrid depth and shadow lines change the finished appearance
Double-door meetingActive leaf, inactive leaf, astragal or meeting-stile detailHardware and closing sequence depend on the meeting arrangement
HardwareLock, flush bolts, hinges, closer and coordinator where neededDouble leaves must close and latch in the intended order

Muntins, glass edges and visual consistency

Confirm whether the grid is a true divided construction, an applied decorative grid or another approved method. The project sample should show the glass edge, muntin proportion, paint or finish, and the view from both sides of the leaf so the installed result matches the desired style.

Double-door hardware and closing sequence

Where both leaves close into one opening, the active and inactive leaves must be clear in the schedule. If a closer or coordinator is required, confirm the hardware location and closing sequence with the selected frame, meeting detail and lock arrangement. Do not add these components after the leaf and frame preparation are fixed.

RFQ checklist for glazed double doors

  • Leaf count, active leaf, inactive leaf and handing.
  • Glass type, thickness or safety requirement and privacy direction.
  • Muntin layout, panel count and finish reference.
  • Frame, threshold, meeting-stile and astragal detail.
  • Hinges, lock, flush bolts, closer and coordinator requirement.
  • Opening size, quantity, packing protection and destination.
