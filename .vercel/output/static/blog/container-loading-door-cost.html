Door Buying Guide

How CBM, weight and container loading affect door cost

Direct Answer

Door cost is affected by the door leaf, frame, hardware, packing method, CBM, gross weight and container utilization. Mixed sizes or many models can reduce loading efficiency, so buyers should confirm packing and loading plans before final order.

Key takeaways

  • Ask for carton size, pallet method, gross weight and CBM per door set.
  • Mixed models should be grouped to avoid packing confusion during loading.
  • Container photos and packing lists are useful proof for B2B buyers.
  • Hardware and frames may ship with the door set or in separate cartons.
  • Destination port and order quantity are needed before freight estimates can be compared.

What to send Visfurn

Send door type, width, height, wall thickness, frame style, hardware list, color or finish, quantity and destination port. If available, include project drawings or a door schedule.
