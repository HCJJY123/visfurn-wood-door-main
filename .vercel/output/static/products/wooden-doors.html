Buyer Answer

How should buyers choose wooden doors for bulk projects?

Confirm the application first: apartments and villas often prioritize finish consistency and frame coordination, hotels may need acoustic cores and lock preparation, while distributors usually need stable specifications, repeatable packaging and clear MOQ planning.

Quote-ready details

  • Door schedule, leaf size and opening quantity
  • Veneer, paint, melamine or PVC finish direction
  • Frame, architrave, wall thickness and hardware prep
  • Sample approval, packing marks and container plan
  • Apartment door schedule and packing guide

Wooden Door Buying FAQ

What information is needed to quote wooden doors for a project?

Send the door schedule, leaf size or rough opening, wall thickness, frame type, finish reference, hardware preparation, quantity and destination port.

Can VISFURN supply wooden door leaf and frame sets together?

Yes. Door leaves, jambs, architraves and related frame details can be reviewed together to reduce installation mismatch risk.

Which finishes are available for wooden doors?

Common directions include veneer, painted finish, melamine and PVC film depending on selected construction and project requirement.

Are wooden doors suitable for hotel and apartment projects?

Yes. Wooden and engineered composite door sets are commonly used for apartments, villas, hotels and serviced residences.

How can buyers compare wooden door supplier quotes?

Compare quotes on the same basis: leaf size or rough opening, frame and jamb scope, finish reference, hardware preparation, quantity, packing, shipping terms and documented exclusions.
