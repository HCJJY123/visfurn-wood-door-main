Custom Interior Wood Door Sets for Canadian Projects

Use a schedule-led review when a Canadian project combines apartment room types, mixed dimensions or complete prehung door sets.

Direct Answer

VISFURN reviews Canadian interior door sets by separating rough openings, leaf sizes, wall thickness, jamb depth and trim requirements across mixed imperial and metric schedules.

Mixed imperial and metric presentation

Canadian project schedules may combine imperial references such as 28x80, 34-inch and 36-inch families with metric drawings or site measurements. Keep the original unit beside the converted value and identify whether each number describes the rough opening, leaf, frame or finished wall.

Frame, jamb and construction choices

Frame and jamb depth should be reviewed against the finished wall build-up. A solid wood frame and an engineered-core leaf are different specification decisions; do not infer one from the other. 35 mm or 45 mm construction should only be used where the selected product data supports it.

Prehung and full door sets can simplify coordination, while slab supply may suit projects with local frame and hardware installation. Acoustic considerations should be stated as a project requirement, not as an unsupported performance claim.
