How to Choose Interior Doors for Apartment Projects: Door Leaf, Frame, Jamb, Hardware and Packing

A B2B buying guide for apartment developers, contractors and importers preparing interior door packages for repeated residential units.

Apartment projectsDoor schedule buying guide

Direct Answer

For apartment projects, choose interior doors by opening schedule, wall thickness, frame and jamb depth, finish consistency, hardware preparation and packing sequence—not by door photo alone.

Start with the opening schedule, not only a product photo

For apartment projects, the right interior door is decided by repeated openings, installation sequence and buyer risk. A useful RFQ should separate bedroom, bathroom, kitchen, utility and corridor doors before choosing finish or hardware.

This prevents mixed wall thickness, wrong frame depth and late hardware changes from becoming site delays.

What buyers should confirm before quotation

  • Door leaf size or rough opening size for each room type.
  • Wall thickness and required jamb width.
  • Frame type, casing/architrave profile and installation method.
  • Finish direction, color tolerance and matching requirement by floor or unit.
  • Hinge, lock body, handle, stopper and closer preparation.
  • Packing sequence by building, floor, room type or opening ID.

Common mistakes in apartment door purchasing

The most common problem is approving a door leaf sample without approving the full door set. For repeat units, the frame, casing, hinge side and packing label are just as important as the visible door finish.

Another issue is using one generic size for all units. Even small variations in bathroom walls or service rooms can require different jamb widths.

Buyer questions

Include opening IDs, quantities, dimensions, wall thickness or frame depth, room exposure, finish reference, hardware schedule, performance notes, delivery destination and any sample-approval requirement.

Approve one complete representative opening—including the leaf, frame, finish, hardware preparation and packing reference—then release the final schedule only after every revision is recorded.
