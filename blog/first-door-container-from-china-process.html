MOQ, Lead Time, Payment and Samples: What Your First Container of Doors from China Actually Looks Like

A practical week-by-week guide for new door importers planning MOQ, samples, production, inspection and freight.

GuideFirst container

Quick answer

A first container of doors from China usually takes 9–14 weeks end to end: 1–2 weeks for specification and quotation, 2–3 weeks for samples, 30–45 days for production after deposit, about 1 week for inspection and loading, then 12–40 days at sea depending on destination.

What is the minimum order quantity for doors?

Door MOQ has three layers: model MOQ, size MOQ and container MOQ. For project buyers, the practical first order is usually one 20GP container or a mixed model container where each model reaches a stable production quantity.

For many interior, WPC or wooden door programs, 50–100 units per model is more realistic than requesting several single-piece styles. A complete 20GP container often holds roughly 180–240 door sets depending on frame, hardware and packing.

How much do samples cost and are they refundable?

A door sample can include a small finish board, a full leaf sample or a door set mock-up with frame and hardware. Buyers normally pay sample cost and air freight. Many suppliers credit sample cost against the first bulk order when the project moves forward.

Who arranges freight — buyer or supplier?

FOB means the supplier handles export packing, inland delivery to the port and export clearance. CIF adds ocean freight and insurance to the destination port. DDP may include customs and local delivery, but it requires a reliable forwarder and clear import responsibility.

What can go wrong on a first container?

  • Handing is marked incorrectly on carton labels.
  • Frame depth does not match wall thickness.
  • Finish approval is not documented before production.
  • Hardware items are missing from the proforma invoice.
  • Door bundles are not grouped by floor, room or installation sequence.
