WPC Door Price per Set: What Does a Frame-Inclusive Quote Cover?

For distributors, importers and apartment or hotel procurement teams comparing interior door proposals before sampling or ordering.

VISFURN · Updated 18 October 2026

Compare the included components before comparing a WPC door price per set. A leaf-and-frame price does not automatically include architraves, handles, locks, hinges, factory hanging, transport to your site or installation. Put those items on the same quotation sheet so an attractive unit price can be evaluated against the opening you actually need.

Frame included does not mean prehung

Confirm whether the supplier delivers the leaf already hung in its specified frame, or sends the leaf, frame and trim as separate components. Ask which hardware is supplied, machined or preinstalled. These are separate scope decisions even when the quotation uses the word “set”.

Use the slab, prehung and full-set guide for the assembly distinction. Then use the packed-CBM and container planning guide after the proposed packaging is defined. Do not calculate freight from the door leaf dimensions alone.

Compare a repeatable opening, then the whole schedule

Choose one typical interior opening and send every supplier the same size basis, finished wall depth, finish direction and hardware requirement. Ask for an itemised response. Keep variations such as bathroom exposure, a different jamb depth, a double leaf or a special performance requirement on separate lines rather than assuming the same unit price applies.

For a mixed apartment or hotel order, list quantities by door mark and delivery phase. Request clarification of the sample, minimum order, production timing, packing and quoted price validity. The final specification and written quotation should identify which decisions remain open.

Start an enquiry with the information you have

You do not need a finished technical schedule to start a conversation. Send the five items below and mark anything uncertain as “to be confirmed”. Additional dimensions and drawings can follow; they are needed before final quotation or production approval.

  1. Product: a linked WPC design or finish reference, plus the intended room use.
  2. Quantity: approximate openings, or quantities by door mark if available.
  3. Destination: country and port or project city when known.
  4. Scope: leaf and frame, trim, hardware and any requested assembly.
  5. Timing and conditions: project dates, sample needs, exposure and specified standards or documents.

If you already have sizes, identify leaf size and rough opening separately and add the finished wall thickness. Send hardware drawings when they are available. For a broader comparison across materials, use the bulk door quotation comparison guide.

WPC price and set-scope questions

Does a WPC door price per set always include the same components?

No. Define the door leaf, frame, architraves, hardware, machining and packing separately. A per-set label alone does not establish the included components or factory assembly.

What do the two VISFURN reference ranges include?

The light woodgrain model is listed at US$40–78 per set including the leaf and frame. The taupe woodgrain model is listed at US$45–98 per set including the door frame. Both use FOB Tianjin Port, China as the published reference basis; final configuration, quantity and exclusions require a written quotation.

Does a frame-inclusive price mean the door is prehung?

No. Frame inclusion and prehung factory assembly are separate decisions. Confirm whether the leaf arrives hung in its frame or whether components are supplied separately for assembly.

Can I start an enquiry before all door dimensions are final?

Yes. Send a product link or finish reference, approximate quantity, application, destination and the information currently available. Mark unconfirmed sizes and hardware as to be confirmed; final price and production approval need the agreed specification.

Can the published reference range be used for every WPC door or a rated hotel door?

No. The ranges belong to the linked designs and their stated supply basis. They do not establish a price for every WPC construction, a custom size, or a tested fire or acoustic assembly.
