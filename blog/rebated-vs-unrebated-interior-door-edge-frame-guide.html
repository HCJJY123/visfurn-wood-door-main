Rebated vs unrebated interior doors: specify the edge, frame and hardware together

For importers, distributors and project contractors comparing wooden, composite or WPC interior-door packages, a front-view photograph is not an edge detail. Before ordering repeated openings, separate the face style from the way the leaf meets its frame or a second leaf.

A flat-faced “flush door” is not automatically an unrebated door. Face design describes what the broad surface looks like. Rebated or unrebated describes an edge and its closing interface. Confirm the supplier's terminology with a dimensioned section, not a name alone. Neither configuration establishes fire, acoustic, moisture or security performance.

Four descriptions that should not be combined into one specification

Terms vary across manufacturers and markets. Use the following as questions for a drawing review, rather than universal definitions that override the proposed system.

Describe appearance and closing geometry separately

Flush face or flush door designA broadly flat face rather than raised or recessed decorative panels. This does not settle the edge or frame arrangement.Face elevation plus a separate edge section.
Unrebated leafAn edge without the overlapping step used in the proposed rebated configuration. The frame stop and closed position still need definition.Leaf-to-jamb section showing both faces, stop, gap and any seal.

An astragal or applied meeting strip should also be drawn explicitly. Do not assume that a photograph of a pair proves an integral rebate, supplied hardware or a particular operating sequence.

Show how the closed leaf sits against the frame

Send a horizontal section through a typical jamb and a section through the head. Label the leaf faces, step where present, frame stop, jamb depth, architrave, wall finish and intended closed position. An appearance described as flush with the frame is another design decision; it is not established solely by calling the leaf unrebated.

Keep rough opening, frame outside size, finished leaf dimensions and the dimensions at a rebate shoulder separate. State which surfaces the measurement references. For replacements, record the existing frame profile and stop rather than copying only the old leaf's width. Use the opening and jamb coordination guide for the broader measurement schedule.

Review hinge and lock templates before machining

Hinge selection depends on more than a rebated/unrebated label. Provide the exact manufacturer, model, current template, mounting positions, proposed leaf construction and the size or weight information requested by the hardware supplier. The hinge axis, receiver, reinforcement and opening clearance must work with the drawn section. A concealed hinge is not evidence that every concealed-hinge model fits the same rebate.

For a latch or lock, coordinate the case, faceplate, backset, strike position, handing and closing interface. On a pair, identify the active leaf and the receiving edge, plus any inactive-leaf bolts or closing-control requirements. The project hardware designer should confirm operation and any accessibility or egress obligations. Use the hardware preparation checklist to record who supplies and who machines each item.

Do not infer sealing or a rating from the overlap

A rebate can be part of a designed closing interface, but the shape alone does not establish a tested acoustic value, smoke leakage result, fire classification or water resistance. Specify the seal position and the complete assembly evidence when the project requires performance documentation. The report or permitted variation must cover the actual construction and relevant components.

Do not solve a mismatch by instructing installers to cut a new rebate, trim a rated leaf or move hardware without the responsible party's approval. Changes can affect the original evidence. The standards and documentation resource explains which assembly details to match. No VISFURN rating or certification is claimed by this guide.

What to send for a bulk interior-door quote

  • Project country, building application, destination port and target approval or delivery date.
  • Door marks and quantities by model, size and handing; flag paired and single openings separately.
  • Plans and elevations plus dimensioned jamb, head and meeting-edge sections where relevant.
  • Leaf construction or material preference, full leaf size and thickness, rough opening, frame size and finished wall thickness.
  • Rebate geometry or an explicit unrebated detail, frame stop, closed-face alignment and seal locations.
  • Finish reference, edge finish and the scope of leaf, frame, trim, hardware, machining and packing.
  • Hardware model numbers and templates, active leaf, opening sequence and any project standards or evidence requirements.

If a section is undecided, label it “for review” and ask for a proposed detail. Do not treat a provisional quote as a fabrication release. For VISFURN's standard bulk procurement, the confirmed minimum is 50 sets per order and 10 sets per model; any project exception needs agreement.

Buyer questions

Is a flush-face door the same as an unrebated door?

No. The first describes face appearance; the second describes the specified edge interface. Request both an elevation and a section.

Can I order a replacement leaf from width and height alone?

That is insufficient to confirm fit. Record the existing frame, stop, rebate or plain edge, hinge and lock positions, handing and the measurement reference faces.

Does VISFURN supply every rebate and hardware combination?

No universal availability is claimed. Send the proposed section, hardware templates and quantity for a configuration-specific review.
