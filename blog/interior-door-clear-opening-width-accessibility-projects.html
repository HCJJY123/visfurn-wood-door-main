Interior Door Clear Opening: Why Leaf Width Is Not Passage Width

A nominal leaf width is not the space people can pass through once the door, stop, frame and hardware are in place.

Write the required clear passage on the door schedule as its own field. The leaf width, rough opening and clear passage describe different things. The finished clear opening depends on the selected door, frame, stop, swing position and installed conditions. Have the project designer confirm the applicable rule and measure the proposed assembly accordingly.

What the U.S. accessibility reference actually measures

For applicable U.S. projects, the 2010 ADA Standards, section 404.2.3, specify a minimum clear width of 32 inches (815 mm) for door openings. For a swinging door, the measurement is between the face of the door and the stop when the door is open 90 degrees. An opening deeper than 24 inches has an additional 36-inch minimum clear-opening provision. These are requirements from that U.S. source, not universal export specifications or a claim that any VISFURN model meets them.

The U.S. Access Board's illustrated guide also addresses maneuvering clearance, thresholds and operable hardware. A width figure alone is insufficient to approve an accessible route. Ask the design authority to determine which openings and standards apply in the destination market, including any more specific local requirements.

Coordinate the swing and adjoining space

Put handing and swing direction on the plan. Check the door at its intended open position; a wall return, door closer, stop or adjoining fixture can change the usable passage or approach. Sliding and folding doors require their own clear-opening measurement basis. Paired doors need the active-leaf and hardware arrangement reviewed against the project's rule rather than simply adding two leaf widths.

For a hotel room, apartment corridor or shared amenity space, include the approach side and nearby circulation in the drawing review. The door supplier can check the proposed leaf and frame against the information received; the architect or responsible project professional must determine compliance for the completed opening.

Translate the requirement into a door RFQ

Provide an opening mark and the required clear passage, not only a nominal leaf size. Send the site-surveyed rough opening, wall depth, finished-floor build-up, frame section, proposed door swing, hardware and any threshold or seal requirement. For repeated room types, flag exceptions on the door schedule; do not assume a single leaf size fits every frame or finish condition.

Ask the bidder to identify the exact leaf, frame, stop and hardware assumptions behind the proposal. If the design is still preliminary, mark the clear-passage calculation and final dimensions as pending designer approval. Any later change in frame profile, closer, threshold or swing should trigger another review before production release.

Buyer handoff checklist

  1. Destination country and the project-design standard named by the responsible designer.
  2. Door marks, opening uses, quantities and required clear passage for each applicable type.
  3. Surveyed rough opening, wall depth and finish-floor reference.
  4. Preferred leaf design, frame/stop section, handing and opening angle.
  5. Hardware, threshold, closer and any performance/document requirements.
  6. Drawing revision, destination, requested delivery phase and project dates.

Mark missing data as pending. Final size and performance commitments require the approved door-set drawing and project review.

Buyer questions

Is an 800 mm leaf an 800 mm clear opening?

No. The usable passage depends on the frame, stop and open leaf geometry. Request a clear-opening dimension for the proposed assembly.

Does every VISFURN interior door satisfy the ADA?

No such blanket claim is made. Applicability and compliance depend on the selected configuration, installed opening and the U.S. project's requirements.

What if the opening is still being designed?

State the required passage and mark leaf/frame dimensions provisional. Ask for a drawing review before the production size is approved.
