Compare the same door scope before comparing the unit price

A low unit price is not useful if one supplier quotes a slab while another includes a prehung set, frame, architrave, hardware preparation or export packing. Put every quote on the same scope before making a buying decision.

Normalize the size and quantity basis

  • State whether dimensions refer to the leaf, frame opening or rough opening.
  • Separate standard, bathroom, service, fire and special openings.
  • Compare quantity by door mark, room type and delivery phase.
  • Record the latest drawing or schedule revision beside the quote.

Use the door schedule takeoff checklist before requesting revisions.

Check frame, jamb and hardware scope

Ask whether the price includes the frame, jamb depth, architrave, threshold, hinge machining, lock preparation, closer reinforcement, viewer or electronic lock preparation. These details often create the largest gap between two apparently similar quotations.

For technical coordination, review the prehung door hardware guide.

Compare finish approval and repeatability

Confirm the finish reference, visible face direction, edge treatment, sample approval process and whether the supplier can reserve or match the finish across repeated rooms. Request exclusions in writing instead of assuming that color matching or frame finishing is included.

Review packing, shipping and exclusions

  • Room, floor, opening ID or batch labels.
  • Foam corners, protective wrapping and pallet or container plan.
  • Destination port, shipping term, lead-time basis and inspection point.
  • Spare hardware, sample cost, taxes, certification documents and installation exclusions.

Quote comparison checklist

Before selecting a supplier, ask for a line-by-line comparison covering product scope, dimensions, frame and hardware, finish, quantity, packing, delivery and exclusions. Then send the same comparison sheet back to each supplier for clarification.

For product routes, compare Wooden Doors, Steel Security Doors and Hospital Doors according to the actual opening schedule.
