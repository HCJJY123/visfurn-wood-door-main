How to Compare Door Supplier Quotes for Bulk Projects

Compare bulk door quotations on the same scope: size basis, door leaf, frame, hardware preparation, finish, packing, shipping terms and exclusions before selecting by unit price.

Direct Answer

Compare bulk door quotations on the same scope: size basis, door leaf, frame, hardware preparation, finish, packing, shipping terms and exclusions before selecting by unit price.

Start with one schedule and one comparison basis

This guide is for importers, distributors and project buyers comparing WPC, wooden and other interior door quotations for apartments, hotels, hospitals or schools. It is most useful after you have a preliminary door schedule and before you select the supplier or approve production.

Send each supplier the same dated schedule, destination, finish references and delivery scope. Ask for one written response against each door mark. A mark identifies an opening configuration; it is more useful than a general description such as “200 wooden doors.” Keep quantities, drawing revisions and unresolved questions together.

Use the door schedule takeoff checklist to organize the input. If the project has no drawings yet, start with a representative opening and approximate quantities, marking unknown fields “to confirm.” An initial budget review is different from an approved production specification.

Build a line-by-line quotation comparison

Copy the following fields into your comparison sheet. For every supplier, record the offered value or reference, whether it is included or excluded, and what still needs confirmation. An empty cell does not mean that an item is included.

Ask for separate lines where a component or service is optional. Compare the leaf-only, frame-and-trim and hardware supply packages before treating two “per set” prices as equivalent.

Check the material and finish behind the product name

WPC describes a material family, while PVC can describe a surface covering on a different leaf construction. Neither a woodgrain photograph nor a matching WPC frame establishes the material inside the leaf. Ask for the selected leaf section, edge detail and frame profile, tied to a model or sample reference. Compare WPC door and frame options with wooden interior door configurations using the actual offered build-up.

Similarly, solid-core does not necessarily mean solid timber. Record the core and face separately. Identify whether a white door is primed for subsequent finishing or supplied with an agreed finished surface, and check whether frames and edges receive the same treatment. Use the primed versus finished interior door guide when that distinction affects the quotation.

Material labels alone do not establish waterproof, fire, smoke or acoustic performance. For bathroom use, state humidity, splash exposure, cleaning method and the threshold or floor transition. For a rated or specialist opening, keep the configuration and documentary review separate from a general material comparison.

Normalize dimensions without assuming a universal allowance

Keep leaf dimensions, outer frame dimensions, rough opening dimensions and usable clear passage in separate fields. A 900 × 2100 mm leaf is not automatically a 900 × 2100 mm rough opening. Frame sections, installation tolerances, seals, hardware and floor conditions affect the final arrangement; the allowances must come from the selected system and approved drawings.

For an existing frame, supply measured rebates, hinge positions, lock preparation and the frame condition before requesting a replacement leaf. Do not assume a door will fit an existing metal frame from nominal dimensions alone. The prehung opening and hardware coordination guide explains the inputs to collect.

For hospital circulation, describe the equipment and movement route and request the required usable opening from the project team. A leaf-width figure alone cannot confirm that the opening meets the project requirement. Review the hospital door range by room function and assembly scope rather than transferring a generic clearance or performance promise between products.

Count complete packages: a 200-opening example

Illustrative quantity example, not a customer order: a schedule contains 120 bedroom openings, 60 bathroom openings and 20 service-room openings. The total is 200 openings. If each requires one leaf and one matching frame, the comparison requires 200 leaves and 200 frame sets. Trim, seals, locks, hinges and other hardware still need their own stated scope and quantities.

A quotation for 200 leaves is therefore not equivalent to a quotation for 200 agreed complete door sets. Ask the leaf-only bidder to price the missing scope, or keep those costs explicitly unresolved. Do not multiply by an invented standard allowance or assume all service openings use the bedroom configuration.

Calculate the comparable supply amount as the sum of each door-mark quantity multiplied by its agreed package price, plus separately quoted one-off items and required options. Compare delivered costs only when the included delivery responsibilities and named destinations are consistent. A missing freight or documentation amount remains “not quoted,” not zero.

For VISFURN wooden, WPC and PVC bulk orders, the confirmed minimum is 50 sets in total and at least 10 sets per model. Count each model separately; reaching 200 overall does not automatically resolve a model below that threshold. Ask about samples or a potential trial rather than assuming an exception, a free sample or a sample-cost credit.

Separate hardware supply from hardware preparation

Machining for a lock is different from supplying that lock. List hinges, locks, closers, seals, viewers, access-control interfaces and glazing individually where required. Record who supplies each item, its approved reference, and who confirms the template and fixing details.

For heavier or frequently used doors, request a review using the selected leaf dimensions, confirmed weight where available, usage and proposed hardware. Do not infer hardware suitability from “solid-core” alone. A change in lock, closer, glazing or frame after quotation can change machining, reinforcement or evidence requirements. Resolve those changes in writing before production release.

Make evidence and sample approval explicit

For each required document, record the issuer, reference, scope and the proposed configuration it supports. A corporate certificate or a report for another model is not proof that the quoted door set meets the project requirement. The standards and documentation resource supports a requirements discussion; it is not a statement that every VISFURN product holds every listed approval.

Agree which physical sample, finish reference, section drawing and hardware template will be reviewed. Tie approvals to the door mark and drawing revision. Mark evidence as pending until it has been reviewed, and identify which unresolved items prevent production release. Do not accept a lower quoted cost by silently removing a required document, component or test condition.

Use the sample approval checklist to record decisions. Sample availability, cost, dispatch and subsequent order terms need a separate written agreement.

Compare packing, cargo data and the delivery starting point

Confirm whether the leaf and frame arrive assembled or separately packed, how hardware is identified, and whether room, floor or opening labels are included. Request package dimensions, counts, gross weight and estimated volume for the agreed configuration before comparing freight proposals. A general statement about container capacity is not a loading plan for your order.

Record the named place or port and which party handles each stage. Clarify what the quotation excludes, including site unloading, storage, installation, local transport, duties or taxes where relevant. Confirm the basis for the proposed delivery date and which approvals or payments must precede production. This guide does not establish a universal lead time or calculate import charges.

Send one concise clarification request

You can use this brief when asking for a revised quotation:

Please revise your quotation against schedule [reference/revision/date] for [application and destination]. Show quantities by door mark; leaf construction and finish; dimension basis; frame and trim; hardware supplied versus machining only; required documents and pending items; packing and cargo data; and delivery/payment basis. Identify every exclusion and alternative separately. Mark unknown items “to confirm” and state which approvals are needed before production.

Share only information you are authorized to disclose. You may redact competing supplier identities, contact details and sensitive prices if your purpose is to compare technical scope.

Review interior door options, then Request a Door Quote Review. Include quantity, intended use, destination port, target delivery date, required standards and the schedule or drawings available. If attaching a file is unsuccessful, email the documents to rose@visfurn.com; do not assume a file has been received until the inquiry is acknowledged.
