Why are WPC doors trending in 2026?

WPC (Wood Plastic Composite) doors have become the preferred choice for eco-conscious developers due to their 100% waterproof performance, termite resistance, and zero-formaldehyde emissions. In commercial projects like hospitals and schools, WPC offers unmatched durability.
