The Architect’s Guide to Choosing STC-Rated Interior Doors for Hotel Projects

How do I choose the best STC-rated doors for a hotel?

To select the optimal STC-rated interior door, architects must balance acoustic performance (typically STC 35-42 for guestrooms) with fire safety requirements and aesthetic finishes. The key is ensuring a "complete acoustic seal," including drop-down bottom seals and perimeter gaskets, as even a 1% gap can reduce noise blocking by 50%.

1. Understanding STC Ratings for Guestrooms

At VISFURN, we understand that guest privacy is the foundation of hotel luxury. STC (Sound Transmission Class) ratings are the standard for measuring noise blocking.

• STC 30-34: Standard doors. Normal speech can be heard but not understood.
• STC 35-39: Loud speech heard but not understood. Recommended for mid-scale hotels.
• STC 40-45: Superior privacy. The gold standard for luxury guestrooms.

2. Beyond the Leaf: The Importance of the Door Package

An STC-rated door is only as good as its installation. A professional hotel interior door package includes acoustic seals and automatic drop seals that activate when the door closes.
