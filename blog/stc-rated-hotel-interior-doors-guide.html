Hotel door acoustic requirements: schedule the complete opening

For a hotel project, begin with the room schedule and the designer’s specified acoustic target. A laboratory result belongs to the tested door assembly and its installation conditions, not to every product that looks similar. VISFURN does not claim an STC rating, fire classification or dual certification for this illustrated product family without a matching report.

Put the correct inputs in the schedule

Record room type, door mark, leaf size, rough opening, wall thickness, frame and jamb, seals and threshold, lock and closer, glazing, opening direction and quantity by type. Note whether corridor, connecting-room and bathroom doors have different targets.

Check the evidence and quote basis

Request the acoustic test method and report, tested assembly drawing, permitted size range, frame and hardware scope, seal schedule and installation instructions. If a fire or smoke requirement also applies, request its separate evidence for the same proposed configuration. Do not transfer a leaf or competitor test to an untested assembly.

Request a project-specific review

Send the drawings, quantities, destination port and planned project dates through the existing door inquiry form. Review the standards and documentation guide for the product and market before treating any performance claim as approved.
