Sourcing Fire-Rated Hotel Guestroom Doors for Vision 2030 Projects in Riyadh

A specification-first guide for hotel teams coordinating fire-door set scope, acoustic expectations, frames, hardware, finishes and export packing before a door package is quoted.

Riyadh & Jeddah hospitality projectsPrepared for B2B door buyers

Direct Answer

For a Riyadh hotel door package, start with the door schedule and verify each opening’s required fire-door set scope, wall condition, frame depth, handing, finish and hardware preparation. Where acoustic performance is required, review the complete assembly—including seals and hardware—rather than selecting a door leaf in isolation.

Why hotel door schedules need a specification-first review

Hotel projects repeat many openings, but the door functions are not identical. Guestroom entrances, bathrooms, service areas and back-of-house openings may use different frames, cores, finishes and hardware. A schedule makes those differences visible before the quote, sample and production stages.

For projects in Riyadh or Jeddah, send the original schedule or drawings together with the finished wall thickness, opening dimensions, destination and installation sequence. A review can then identify what is already confirmed and what still needs a drawing, hardware template or approved sample.

Confirm the fire-door requirement as a complete set

Terms such as FD30 or FD60 may appear in a project specification, but buyers should not treat a rating as a generic label that can be added to any door. Confirm the exact required standard, authority or project requirement, test or evidence scope, wall construction, frame, seals, closer and hardware configuration before approval.

  • Identify which opening marks require fire performance and which do not.
  • Confirm the required evidence and the exact configuration it covers.
  • Check frame material, depth, perimeter seals, threshold details and closer requirements.
  • Keep the approved construction aligned with the project’s hardware preparation.

Coordinate acoustic privacy with fire and hardware details

Guestroom privacy is affected by gaps around the leaf as well as the leaf construction. When an acoustic target is included, review the perimeter seals, bottom seal or threshold condition, frame fit, hinges, lock preparation and installation tolerance as part of one system. A room-door quotation is more reliable when it records these details opening by opening.

Lock, hinge and handing information should arrive before production

Hotel locks and hinges are not finishing details. Their templates determine machining, reinforcement and handing. Request the hardware schedule or approved templates early, particularly where electronic locks, viewers, closers, latch guards or room-number details are used.

  • Door mark, room type and floor or zone.
  • Leaf size, opening direction and handing.
  • Finished wall thickness and frame depth.
  • Hinge quantity, size, position and finish.
  • Lock case, backset, cylinder, electronic-lock or closer template.
  • Finish reference, veneer direction and approved sample requirement.

Use packing logic that matches the installation sequence

Export packing should protect finished faces, edges, frames and hardware during shipment and site handling. For a multi-floor hotel, labels can follow room type, floor, opening mark or delivery batch so the installation team does not need to re-sort a full container at site.

State whether frames, leaves, architraves and hardware need separate grouping. This is also the moment to confirm the destination port and whether the project requires staged delivery.

Riyadh hotel RFQ checklist

  • Door schedule or drawing with each opening mark.
  • Required fire-door set scope and documentation request where applicable.
  • Acoustic target and seal or threshold requirement where applicable.
  • Opening size, finished wall thickness, frame depth and handing.
  • Hardware schedule or approved lock, hinge and closer templates.
  • Finish reference, sample approval route and mock-up requirement.
  • Destination, delivery batches and packing labels.

Buyer questions

Confirm the opening list, required fire-door set scope, required evidence, wall construction, frame depth, leaf size, handing, hardware template, finish, acoustic target if any, destination and packing sequence.

It can be reviewed as a complete door set, but the required performance and supporting documents must be confirmed for the exact configuration, frame, seals, hardware and site condition.

A door schedule links every opening to its size, function, handing, frame, finish and hardware. It lets the supplier identify missing details and quote repeated door types consistently.

Project packing can be grouped by room type, floor, opening mark or installation sequence, with protection for finished faces, edges, frames and separately packed hardware as required.
