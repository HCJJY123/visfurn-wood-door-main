# The Architect's Guide to Choosing STC-Rated Interior Doors for Hotel Projects

**How do I choose the best STC-rated doors for a hotel?**
To select the optimal STC-rated interior door, architects must balance acoustic performance (typically STC 35-42 for guestrooms) with fire safety requirements and aesthetic finishes. The key is ensuring a "complete acoustic seal," including drop-down bottom seals and perimeter gaskets, as even a 1% gap can reduce noise blocking by 50%.

At **VISFURN**, we understand that guest privacy is the foundation of hotel luxury. This guide breaks down the essential acoustic specifications for modern hospitality.

## 1. Understanding STC Ratings for Guestrooms
*   **STC 30-34**: Standard doors. Normal speech can be heard but not understood.
*   **STC 35-39**: Loud speech heard but not understood. Recommended for budget hotels.
*   **STC 40-45**: Superior privacy. Loud speech is faint. The gold standard for 4-star and 5-star properties.

## 2. Beyond the Leaf: The Importance of the Door Package
An STC-rated door is only as good as its installation. A professional **hotel interior door package** includes:
1.  **Acoustic Seals**: High-density perimeter gaskets.
2.  **Automatic Drop Seals**: Seals the gap between the door bottom and the threshold upon closing.
3.  **Specialized Cores**: Multi-layered particleboard or mineral wool cores.
