Hospital Door RFQ Checklist: Hygiene, Hardware, Vision Panels and Room Zones

Direct Answer

A hospital door RFQ should separate room zones and define hygiene, vision, impact, hardware, frame, operation and performance requirements before quotation.

Start with room zones, not one hospital-wide door type

Patient rooms, wards, clean corridors, utility rooms, imaging areas and service openings can have different cleaning, access, visibility, impact and shielding requirements. Separate the room schedule before selecting a common door model.

Define hygiene and maintenance conditions

Record the cleaning routine, surface exposure, sealed edges, frame material, impact plate requirement and hardware environment. A smooth door leaf alone does not define how the complete opening will be maintained.

Compare moisture-resistant options on the WPC Doors page where wet cleaning is part of the project brief.

Coordinate vision panels, impact protection and hardware

  • Vision panel size, position and safety-glazing requirement.
  • Kick plates, push plates, impact protection and edge protection.
  • Hinges, closers, access control, handles and handing.
  • Frame depth, wall thickness, threshold and clear opening.
  • Hermetic sliding, controlled swing or standard hinged operation.

Include performance and document boundaries

Identify any clean-room, pressure, fire, acoustic or radiation-shielding requirement from the project drawings. A supplier should review the leaf, frame, seals, hardware and glazing as a coordinated system; do not infer a certification from a product name alone.

Hospital door RFQ fields

  • Room zone, door mark and quantity.
  • Leaf size or rough opening, wall thickness and frame profile.
  • Surface, edge, cleaning and impact-protection requirements.
  • Vision panel, access-control, closer and hinge details.
  • Sliding or swing layout, threshold and clear opening.
  • Performance documents, drawing revision, packing labels and destination port.
