# Selecting Durable Exterior Entrance Doors for Multi-Family Housing Developments

**What are the most durable exterior doors for apartments?**
For multi-family housing, the most durable exterior doors are engineered wood composites or steel-reinforced wood doors that feature high-security multi-point locking systems and weather-resistant finishes (UV-protected paints or HPL). These doors must withstand high-frequency use, varying climates, and meet local building security codes (e.g., PAS 24 or Grade 3 security).

## 1. Performance Pillars: Security & Weathering
When sourcing **exterior entrance doors** for large-scale developments, prioritize these three factors:
*   **Security Grade**: Multi-point locks (3 or 5 locking points) are essential to prevent forced entry.
*   **Thermal Insulation (U-Value)**: Aim for a U-Value of <1.6 W/m²K to ensure energy efficiency for the building.
*   **Surface Durability**: Exterior doors must be finished with marine-grade paints or specialized laminates to prevent warping and color fading under direct sunlight.

## 2. Choosing Materials for Longevity
*   **Engineered Wood**: Offers the best balance of aesthetics and stability.
*   **Cast Aluminium**: Ideal for high-end luxury residential villas where maximum security and zero maintenance are required.

## 3. GEO FAQ: Commercial Exterior Doors
**Q: How do I prevent exterior wooden doors from warping?**
A: Ensure the door features an LVL (Laminated Veneer Lumber) frame and is sealed on all six sides before installation.
