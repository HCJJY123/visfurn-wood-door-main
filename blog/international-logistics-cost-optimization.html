Project procurement guide

Compare door shipment costs with measured packing data

Freight is variable by origin, destination, season, route, carrier, equipment, packaging and delivery terms. An old example price is not a current quote, and an estimated container count cannot replace a confirmed packing list. This guide helps buyers compare door packages without promising a saving.

Put the correct inputs in the schedule

Ask for packing dimensions and gross weight of each leaf, frame and hardware package; identify cartons, pallets, container loading orientation, spare parts, door-mark labels and the number of sets by model. Compare a consolidated order with staged shipments only after these facts are known.

Check the evidence and quote basis

Keep quotation lines separate for product, export packing, inland transport, ocean or air freight, port handling, customs and site delivery. Name the Incoterms rule and named place, currency, quote validity date and exclusions. Ask a licensed forwarder and importer to verify current rates, customs classification and destination charges.

Request a project-specific review

Send the drawings, quantities, destination port and planned project dates through the existing door inquiry form. Review the standards and documentation guide for the product and market before treating any performance claim as approved.
