Selecting Durable Exterior Entrance Doors for Multi-Family Housing Developments

What are the most durable exterior doors for apartments?

For multi-family housing, the most durable exterior doors are engineered wood composites or steel-reinforced wood doors that feature high-security multi-point locking systems and weather-resistant finishes. These doors must withstand high-frequency use and meet local building security codes.

1. Performance Pillars: Security & Weathering

When sourcing exterior entrance doors for large-scale developments, prioritize these three factors:

• Security Grade: Multi-point locks are essential to prevent forced entry.
• Thermal Insulation: Aim for a low U-Value to ensure energy efficiency.
• Surface Durability: Exterior doors must be finished with UV-protected paints to prevent warping.
