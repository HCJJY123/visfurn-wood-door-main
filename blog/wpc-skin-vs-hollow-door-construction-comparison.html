WPC Skin Doors vs Hollow WPC Doors: Compare the Construction

For importers, distributors and apartment or hotel contractors comparing door proposals before sample approval.

VISFURN procurement guide · 23 October 2026

Compare WPC doors by the proposed leaf build-up, fixing zones, finish and complete-set scope. “WPC skin,” “hollow WPC” and “solid WPC” are supplier descriptions; they do not establish equivalent construction, water resistance, acoustic performance or price. Request an identified section and sample before approving repeated openings.

What do WPC skin, hollow and assembled descriptions mean?

Start by asking the supplier to define its terminology. A skin describes a facing component; a hollow description refers to internal voids; an assembled description identifies a way of joining components. These terms answer different questions and should not be treated as a universal quality ranking.

Compare the same opening before comparing the price

Choose one door mark from the project schedule. Send the same room use, leaf dimensions, finished wall depth, finish reference and hardware requirement with each request. Specify whether the price includes the frame, architraves, hinges, lock, machining, assembly and packing.

Keep differences visible. A leaf with a different finish, a separate frame or omitted hardware is not the same quotation scope even if both lines say “WPC door set.” Use the WPC price-per-set scope guide to compare inclusions after the construction has been identified.

Resolve hinge and lock fixing before machining

Identify the selected hardware model and its preparation drawing. Ask where hinge screws, lock fixings, a closer or any other specified fitting will be anchored in the offered leaf and frame. The reply should identify the fixing detail and any required insert or reinforcement rather than relying on the word “solid.”

For a hollow or built-up proposal, ask how the internal profile and edge construction relate to the proposed cut-outs. Confirm leaf thickness, lock location, handing and the hardware template before repeated machining. Do not approve a screw size, recess depth or hinge load from a generic material comparison.

The WPC bathroom hardware guide covers the rest of the opening. The selected product and hardware documentation must settle the actual fixing method.

Separate leaf construction from wet-area and performance approval

Record whether the room involves humidity, splashes, repeated wet cleaning or another exposure. Request confirmation for the proposed leaf, edges, finish, frame, joints and hardware in that condition. A WPC facing description does not tell the buyer what the rest of the assembly contains.

For required fire, smoke, acoustic or emissions performance, request documentation identifying the offered configuration and the applicable test or approval scope. A hollow leaf is not automatically unsuitable, and a solid description is not evidence of a rating. Have the project's responsible reviewer assess the actual requirements.

Keep cutting permission separate as well. Before ordering a size that needs site alteration, request the model-specific trimming and cut-edge instructions. The door-bottom and floor-clearance guide explains which dimensional references need coordination.

Make the construction traceable to the approved sample

Request a representative section, identified sample and drawing for the quoted model. Record the leaf build-up, finish, edges, frame profile, hardware preparation and revision together. Photographs support the record but do not replace the agreed sample and written specification.

For a mixed apartment or hotel order, group quantities by the approved door type. If a proposed core, profile, finish or fixing detail changes after sampling, review the changed item before releasing the affected openings. Use the WPC sample-to-batch approval guide to control that later approval stage.

Send these details for a construction and quotation review

  1. Room use, moisture or cleaning exposure, project country and approximate quantity.
  2. Product link or finish reference, plus any supplier construction description you want to compare.
  3. Actual leaf size, finished wall depth and frame or trim requirement, if known.
  4. Hinge, lock and other hardware models or functions, with drawings when available.
  5. Required performance documents, sample requirements and project dates.
  6. Destination port or project city, delivery phase and the requested packing or assembly format.

Send the information already available and identify unresolved decisions. The initial enquiry can begin before the final construction is selected; the approved model and dimensions must be confirmed before production release. For alternative material routes, compare the interior door collection.

WPC construction questions from project buyers

Is a WPC skin door the same as an extruded hollow WPC door?

No. A facing used in a built-up leaf and an extruded profile can describe different constructions. Request the offered model's section, core or cavities, edges and fixing details rather than assuming the two labels mean the same product.

Does a solid WPC description prove better acoustic performance?

No. Ask for evidence for the proposed complete door assembly, including the frame, seals, hardware and installation conditions. The material or core label alone does not establish an acoustic result.

Can I select WPC door hinges from a catalogue photograph?

A photograph can show a design direction. Hardware selection and machining need the actual leaf and frame construction, dimensions, fixing provisions and the hardware manufacturer's requirements.

Do all WPC constructions have the same trimming allowance?

Do not assume a common allowance. Confirm permitted edges, limits, machining method and any cut-edge treatment for the selected model before allowing site adjustment.

Can VISFURN quote before my construction specification is final?

Start with the product reference, application, approximate quantity, destination and available dimensions. Ask for a configuration review and identify open decisions. A final quotation and production release need an agreed specification.
