Door Size Standards: Inches, Millimetres and Opening Measurements

Convert door sizes from inches to mm, distinguish leaf, frame and rough opening measurements, and send a quote-ready schedule for bulk project doors.

Global buyersPrepared for B2B door buyers

Direct Answer

30 × 80 inches converts exactly to 762 × 2032 mm. If those are door-leaf dimensions, they are not the required wall opening. Specify the leaf, frame outside dimensions, rough opening and finished wall thickness separately; confirm fitting allowances from the approved door-set drawing.

Use 1 inch = 25.4 mm for conversion. A unit conversion does not establish a local building requirement, accessible clear opening or product approval.

Why does a 30 × 80 door not fit a 762 × 2032 wall opening?

The leaf is the moving panel. A complete frame takes additional space, and fitting clearances depend on the selected door set and site conditions. If a buyer measures wall to wall, label that measurement as an opening measurement—not as an approved door-leaf size.

Do not subtract a generic allowance from every opening. Share the measurements and frame detail for review, then approve the resulting leaf and frame dimensions. For a replacement project, record the retained frame and existing leaf separately.

Separate leaf-only and complete-set quotation scope

Dimensions alone do not define a complete door package. State whether you need the leaf only or the leaf with frame, jamb and architrave. Separate hardware supplied from hardware preparation, and provide the lock, hinge or closer drawings where applicable.

Include finish, room exposure, project-specific documentation requirements and the destination. If goods are to be delivered to a consolidation warehouse before export, distinguish that receiving address from the final destination; do not assume freight, unloading or installation is included.

How to order a mixed-size door package

Keep one schedule line for each opening type and flag exceptions by opening ID. List dimensions, units, quantity, handing, frame depth, finish, hardware set and delivery phase. Mixed dimensions do not need a universal size-count limit; packing and loading must follow the confirmed configuration and quantities.

If a dimension changes after quotation, issue a dated revision identifying the affected openings. Do not treat a preliminary measurement as a production release.

Review the door schedule quotation checklist and jamb depth and wall-thickness guide before uploading the schedule.

What changes between country and project requirements?

Unit conventions do not establish the applicable local code, fire-door approval, acoustic target or accessible clear opening. Ask the project’s responsible designer or reviewer for those requirements and provide the corresponding specifications with the schedule.

Confirm custom dimensions, configuration and quantity before pricing. This guide does not promise a universal MOQ, stock size, manufacturing tolerance, lead time or fixed rough-opening allowance.

FAQ

Door measurement questions

What is a 30 × 80 inch door size in millimetres?

It converts exactly to 762 × 2032 mm. If that describes the leaf, confirm frame outside dimensions, rough opening and fitting clearances separately.

Should I send the old door size or wall-opening size?

Send both when available and label each measurement. For a replacement, also identify whether the existing frame is retained or replaced, with wall thickness and floor details.

Can I use one rough-opening allowance for every door?

No. Confirm allowances against the selected frame, door set and approved installation drawing. Do not reduce wall-to-wall measurements using a generic rule.

Can a bulk door order contain different sizes?

Provide a separate schedule line for each opening type, with dimensions, units, quantity, handing, frame scope and delivery phase. Ask for review of the proposed configuration rather than assuming a fixed limit.

Does a leaf width prove accessibility or fire compliance?

No. Clear opening and any fire or other performance requirement depend on the specified configuration and applicable project approval, not on a leaf dimension alone.
