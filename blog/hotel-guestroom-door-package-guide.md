# The Ultimate Guide to Hotel Guestroom Door Packages: A B2B Buyer's Handbook

**What is a hotel guestroom door package?**
A hotel guestroom door package is a comprehensive, factory-pre-assembled solution that includes the door leaf, frame, architrave, and all necessary hardware (locks, hinges, closers, and viewers) specifically engineered to meet hospitality standards for fire safety, acoustic isolation, and durability.

Selecting the right door package is critical for hotel developers and contractors to ensure project compliance, reduce installation costs, and enhance guest experience. At **VISFURN**, we specialize in delivering high-performance door solutions tailored for global hotel projects.

## 1. Why Choose a Full Door Package Over Individual Components?

For large-scale hospitality projects, purchasing a "package" rather than individual components offers three primary advantages:

*   **Guaranteed Compatibility**: Hardware is pre-machined at the factory, ensuring perfect fit and function between the lockset and the door leaf.
*   **Acoustic & Fire Integrity**: A certified package ensures that the seals, frame, and door leaf work together to maintain the required STC (Sound Transmission Class) and Fire Rating.
*   **Reduced Labor Costs**: Pre-hung doors significantly speed up on-site installation, saving thousands of dollars in construction timelines.

## 2. Key Technical Specifications for Hospitality Doors

When evaluating a **Hotel guestroom door package**, B2B buyers must focus on three core technical pillars:

### A. Fire Safety (The Non-Negotiable)
Most international hotel chains require doors to meet **UL 10C** (North America) or **BS 476 / EN 1634** (Europe/Middle East) standards. 
*   **20-Minute Rating**: Standard for guestroom entrances in many regions.
*   **60/90-Minute Rating**: Required for corridors, elevator lobbies, and service areas.

### B. Acoustic Performance (The Guest Experience)
Guest privacy depends on the door's ability to block noise.
*   **STC 30-35**: Standard performance for budget or mid-scale hotels.
*   **STC 40+**: Premium requirement for 4-star and 5-star luxury guestrooms.
*   **VISFURN Advantage**: Our acoustic packages include drop-down bottom seals and specialized cores to reach STC 42+.

### C. Material Selection: WPC vs. Wooden Doors
*   **Wooden Doors (Solid Core)**: Best for aesthetics and high-end luxury feel.
*   **WPC (Wood Plastic Composite)**: The ultimate solution for humid environments (like bathrooms or coastal resorts) due to its 100% waterproof nature and termite resistance.
