Pre-Shipment Video Inspection for Door Orders: What Buyers Should Check

A practical pre-shipment video inspection checklist for door buyers covering quantity, finish, hardware preparation, packing and container-loading readiness.

Quality ControlPre-shipment inspection

Quick answer

A pre-shipment video inspection should verify the approved door sample, opening marks, quantity, finish consistency, frame and hardware preparation, function where applicable, packing protection and shipment readiness against the final schedule before the order is dispatched.

What a pre-shipment video can verify

Video is most useful when it follows a buyer-approved inspection checklist. It can show the production area, representative door sets, finish comparison, hardware preparation, opening labels, packed quantities and loading readiness. It does not replace every possible test, but it provides a traceable review point before dispatch.

Inspection checklist by stage

StageWhat to showReference
Approved referenceSample, finish panel and hardware templateSigned sample or controlled approval record
Finished doorsRepresentative leaves, frames and key opening typesFinal schedule and opening IDs
FunctionHinges, lock preparation, swing or matching components where applicableHardware schedule
PackingCorner protection, wrapping, labels and groupingPacking specification
DispatchCounted pallets or bundles and loading readinessPacking list and shipment plan

Hardware and function checks

For a representative sample of each relevant opening type, show hinge alignment, lock or latch preparation, closer or seal coordination and the visible finish around machining areas. If hardware is supplied separately, show the labeling and package grouping against the schedule rather than implying that a leaf has been factory-fitted.

Packing and dispatch readiness

Request a view of the packing protection, bundle or pallet labels, room or opening grouping and final count. For container orders, coordinate the video with the packing list and loading sequence so the buyer can match what was reviewed with what is prepared for dispatch.

Buyer video-inspection request

  • Final schedule and approved sample reference available during the review.
  • Representative examples of each leaf, frame, finish and hardware-preparation type.
  • Close views of key details and any special opening configuration.
  • Packing, count and labeling review before loading.
  • Clear record of any correction, replacement or outstanding action.

Buyer questions

It depends on the buyer’s risk controls and project requirements. A video review is a useful evidence layer, but buyers should decide separately whether independent inspection or testing is needed.

Show the approved sample reference, representative door sets, finish and hardware-preparation details, packing protection, labels, quantities and shipment readiness against the final schedule.

Review representative examples of each material, finish, opening type and special hardware configuration, plus the final count and packing grouping for the whole order.

Complete the product and packing review before loading, then capture loading readiness or the loading sequence when it is part of the agreed shipment evidence.

Record the affected opening IDs or quantities, agree the corrective action and confirm that the revised condition is checked before the shipment is released.
