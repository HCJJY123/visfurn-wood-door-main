Prepare the package for review

For apartments, hotels, schools, healthcare facilities and commercial openings, provide door mark, quantity per type, leaf and rough-opening dimensions, finished wall thickness, frame and architrave scope, handing, hardware preparation, finish and destination port. Mark fire, smoke, acoustic or moisture requirements separately. A tested door leaf cannot be assumed to cover an untested frame or hardware combination.

For phased delivery, include room or floor labels, packing groups and the target site dates. Review the door schedule quotation guide and standards and document checks before requesting a price.
