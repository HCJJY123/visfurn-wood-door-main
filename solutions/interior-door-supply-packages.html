Start with the supply scope, then choose the door design

An interior door quotation should identify the door leaf, frame, architraves, hardware, machining, finish state and packing separately. A door photographed inside a frame does not establish what is included in the price. Use this page to prepare a comparable RFQ for wooden, PVC-faced or WPC interior doors.

Three package options to discuss

These are RFQ routes, not fixed-price bundles. Availability and minimum quantities depend on the selected model and configuration.

Door leaf only

For buyers using a separately sourced or existing frame. Send the frame profile, rebate or stop detail, hinge locations and lock preparation. A nominal leaf size alone cannot confirm compatibility.

Ask about a leaf-only scope

Leaf, frame and trim

For buyers coordinating the visible opening as one package. Identify frame material, finished wall thickness, jamb depth and architraves for one or both wall faces. Confirm assembly and hardware separately.

Specified hardware package

For projects requiring a coordinated hardware schedule. List locks, latches, handles, hinges, seals, stops and closers as applicable. Separate supplied hardware from factory drilling or routing.

White primed and white finished are different buying requirements

White primed means a primed surface intended for an agreed subsequent finishing process. White finished means a final specified white surface is part of the supply approval. Neither term defines the core, frame material or assembly. State whether the leaf, frame and trim share the same finish requirement.

For distributor stock, discuss local finishing capability, preferred size range and packaging. For installation projects, discuss approved final color, sheen, edge treatment and protection. Model suitability must be reviewed before a finish option is offered.

Compare white primed and white finished doors or start with the white moulded interior door design reference. The product image does not confirm a primer-only supply option.

Assembly, leaf size and wall thickness need separate decisions

Knock-down describes components supplied for later assembly. Prehung describes a leaf hung in its frame within the agreed assembly scope. “Complete set” describes an inclusion list; it does not by itself confirm that the set is preassembled or includes every hardware item.

  • Label dimensions as door-leaf size, frame outside size or rough opening. State the unit and width × height order.
  • Give the finished wall thickness and identify any existing frame that must be retained.
  • Use a handing drawing viewed from a clearly identified side. Agree hinge and lock positions before machining.
  • Ask for packed dimensions and weight for the selected assembly before comparing freight.

Use the slab, prehung and full-set guide and the leaf size, rough opening and jamb-depth checklist. Do not derive manufacturing dimensions from a room photograph.

Match the material description to the actual parts

A wood-based leaf supplied with a WPC frame should be described by both components. It should not be assumed to have the same construction as a WPC leaf and WPC frame. Ask for the selected leaf cross-section, core description, facing, frame material and edge detail.

  • Interior doors: compare white moulded and PVC-faced design directions.
  • Wooden doors: review construction, decorative finish and project configuration.
  • WPC doors: identify leaf and frame materials, surface system and intended room conditions.

Fire resistance, acoustics and other performance requirements need their own review. A supply-package name or photograph is not performance evidence. Use the standards and documentation review for the required assembly and market.

Professional samples and trial orders

A finish chip, cross-section, working leaf/frame sample and commercial trial answer different questions. Tell us which decision you need to make and who will approve it. Samples and smaller commercial trials are reviewed separately for feasibility, pricing, packing and freight; acceptance is not automatic.

Standard wooden, WPC and PVC batch orders: 50 sets total, with at least 10 sets per model. A sample discussion does not change these batch-order terms. Do not assume a sample charge will be credited against a future order unless the quotation states it.

Send a scope that can be quoted line by line

  1. Buyer and application: distributor stock, apartment, hotel or other interior project; destination country and port.
  2. Door schedule: quantities by model and opening ID; dimensional basis, wall thickness and handing.
  3. Construction and finish: leaf/core requirement, frame and trim material, white primed or finished state, reference sample.
  4. Inclusions: leaf, frame, trim, seals, hardware, preparation and assembly. Identify buyer-supplied items.
  5. Delivery scope: export packing, carton labels, agreed trade term, named place or port and target delivery window.

Request product value, sample costs if relevant, packing, freight and destination services as identifiable quote lines. Confirm what the quoted delivery scope covers before comparing a goods-only offer with a delivered offer. Import taxes, clearance and local delivery responsibilities need explicit agreement.
