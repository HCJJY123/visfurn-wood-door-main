Order coordination

Agree the details before production.

A reference image is a useful starting point. The approved schedule, drawings, sample and written supply scope define what is to be made and packed.

  1. Review the brief

    Share your application, door marks, quantities, sizes or drawings, destination and project dates.

  2. Define the package

    Separate the leaf, frame, trim, finished wall thickness, opening direction and hardware requirements.

  3. Confirm the reference

    Agree the finish, construction, drawings and any sample approval needed for the proposed order.

  4. Review the checks

    Define dimensional, appearance, hardware-preparation and documentation requirements for your configuration.

  5. Coordinate packing

    Agree protection, labels, handling, shipment scope and destination details in the quotation.

Packing is part of the quoted scope.

Protection needs depend on the finish, package, handling route and installation plan. Confirm pallet dimensions, wrapping, edge protection, labels and loading information for the selected door package.

For phased projects, provide the building, floor or room identification you need on the packing list. Ask for the agreed quantities and relevant packing photographs when reviewing dispatch.

Bulk wooden, WPC and PVC door orders start at 50 sets in total, with at least 10 sets per model. Discuss professional samples or smaller commercial trials with the team before preparing the full order schedule.

Quality & technical review

Make the approval criteria specific.

For a project order, the review should connect the approved specification to the actual leaf, frame, finish and hardware scope. Tell us which checkpoints and supporting documents your buyer or contractor needs.

  • Dimensions: distinguish the rough opening, leaf size, clear passage and finished wall thickness.
  • Appearance: use the agreed colour, grain, surface and sample reference.
  • Coordination: identify handing, frame depth, hinges, lock preparation and other hardware interfaces.
  • Dispatch: confirm quantity, labels, protection and the packing-list scope.

Material & project documents

Request documents for the proposed configuration.

Include any material-emission, sourcing, fire, acoustic or other project requirements in your enquiry. Request the relevant test report or certificate and review its holder, issue date, validity, product scope and configuration before approval.

A document for one board, material or door configuration does not automatically cover every finished door. Fire and acoustic requirements must be reviewed together with the specified frame, seals, hardware and installation conditions.
