Door standards and documentation: check the exact door set before ordering

A project door is not compliant because a standard appears on a catalogue page. Ask for evidence matching the quoted leaf, frame, seals, glazing, hardware, size range and installation condition. VISFURN does not claim that every listed door has passed the standards below.

Request a door quote and document reviewPrepare a door schedule

Start with the opening, not a certificate name

For each door mark, state the project country and approving authority, room use, rough opening and leaf size, finished wall thickness, leaf and frame construction, glazing, handing, hinges, latch or lock, closer, seals and threshold. Add the required fire, smoke, acoustic, moisture or emissions target only where the project calls for it. Share the latest drawings and expected quantity by model.

Evidence rule: A test report or certificate for one leaf or assembly does not automatically cover another frame, glazing, hardware set, size or wall construction. Ask for the report, classification or listing scope, any permitted variations, and installation instructions for the proposed set.

Market questions to put in the RFQ

United States

For finished goods containing regulated hardwood plywood, MDF or particleboard, ask the importer and supplier to determine TSCA Title VI obligations, panel certification, labels and records. Check California CARB requirements where relevant. For a fire opening, identify the locally required assembly, test or listing route, such as UL 10C, and installation obligations under NFPA 80. Specify ANSI/BHMA hardware grades only when the project requires them.

EPA composite wood guidance · UL fire door guide

United Kingdom

FD30, FD30S, FD60 and FD60S describe requested fire resistance and, where “S” is used, smoke control; they are not VISFURN certifications. Ask for evidence applicable to the complete doorset. BS 476-22 and BS EN 1634-1 are fire test routes; BS EN 1634-3 addresses smoke leakage. Confirm current local building guidance, transitional rules and the accepted assessment route with the project authority. CE and UKCA marking rules depend on the product and the part of the UK market.

Approved Document B · GB construction product rules

European Union

First identify whether the item is an external or internal doorset and its intended use. EN 1634-1 is a fire test method; EN 16034 addresses fire or smoke performance and is used with an applicable product standard such as EN 14351-1 for external pedestrian doorsets. Do not assume an internal doorset has the same harmonised route. CE marking and a Declaration of Performance apply only under the relevant harmonised standard or assessment route, with evidence for the marketed configuration.

EU harmonised standard notice · CPR overview

Australia

Ask the designer or authority whether the opening needs a fire-resistance level (FRL). For fire-resistant doorsets, confirm the project’s use of AS 1905.1 and the applicable AS 1530.4 test evidence. The required assembly, wall type, dimensions and hardware must be within the documented scope. These references do not make a normal interior door fire rated.

Standards Australia: AS 1905.1

Canada

For relevant composite wood materials and finished goods, check Canada’s formaldehyde-emissions rules and the supplier statements or records expected from an importer. For a fire door project, ask the local authority whether CAN/ULC-S104 and related assembly documentation apply. Province, building use and complete opening details matter.

Health Canada guidance · ULC building materials

Other project evidence

FSC or PEFC chain-of-custody claims need an applicable, current certificate and a traceable claim on the quoted product. E1 or E0 is incomplete without the exact test standard and material scope. For acoustic, WPC water absorption or dimensional stability, request the actual method, specimen construction and result. Local acceptance must be checked separately.
