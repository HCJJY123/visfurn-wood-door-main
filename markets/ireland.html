VISFURN · B2B door procurement

Project doors for Ireland

For Irish apartment, hotel or commercial work, confirm whether each opening is a normal interior door or a regulated fire or smoke doorset. Do not assume that a product label or test for a different configuration meets the project specification.

Opening schedule

List door marks, quantities, leaf and rough-opening sizes, finished wall thickness, handing and frame details.

EU project evidence

Ask the design team which applicable Irish rules, product standard, CE/DoP route or fire and smoke evidence is required for the actual doorset. UKCA is not a substitute for checking Ireland’s EU market route.

Quote scope

Specify finish, ironmongery, seals, packing labels, destination port and document approval date.

Request an Ireland project quote

Send your door schedule or drawings, quantity, project location and required documents. Suitability and available evidence must be reviewed for the actual proposed door configuration.
