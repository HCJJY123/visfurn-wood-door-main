Door Package Planning for US Multifamily and Commercial Projects

A US multifamily, hotel or commercial package can include many repeated openings as well as mixed door types. A usable schedule identifies opening ID, room type, door type, quantity, handing, finish, frame, hardware preparation and packing phase. This lets the project quotation be organized around the complete opening rather than an isolated door leaf.

Questions from USA project buyers

What should a US contractor include in a door schedule?

Include the opening ID, room type, quantity, rough opening or leaf dimensions, handing, finish, frame or jamb scope, hardware preparation, glazing where applicable, destination and packing phase.

Should buyers send rough-opening size, door-leaf size, or both?

Send both whenever available. Rough opening, door leaf, frame size and finished wall thickness are coordinated as separate project details before production information is confirmed.

How should hardware templates be coordinated before machining?

Share the buyer-supplied hardware schedule, approved templates, handing and machining information. Lock, hinge, closer, access-control or other preparation is confirmed against that project information.

Can mixed apartment or hotel door types be packed by opening schedule?

A mixed apartment or hotel door package can be reviewed against its opening schedule, product scope, dimensions, frame and hardware requirements, finish and packing plan. Opening, room and phase labels should be identified in the schedule.

What project documentation should be reviewed before quotation?

Useful documents include architectural drawings, the door and finish schedules, hardware schedule or templates, project specification, required standards and any authority requirements supplied by the buyer.

How are imperial and metric dimensions reconciled?

Imperial drawings can be coordinated into production dimensions, but rough opening, leaf size, frame size, wall thickness and hardware positions must be confirmed for the project rather than assumed from a simple conversion.

Can the RFQ begin before every hardware model is final?

Yes. An initial quotation review can begin with the available schedule and scope. Missing hardware models, templates or machining details should be clarified before final production information is approved.

How should phased delivery requirements be shown in the schedule?

Identify the required packing or delivery phase, opening IDs, room labels, quantities and destination details so the packing plan can be reviewed with the approved project configuration.
