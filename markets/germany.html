Project Document and Revision Control

  1. Issued documents identify drawing number, schedule version, revision date, hardware reference and finish reference.
  2. Technical review clarifies open points and records the latest approved dimensions and approval status.
  3. Revised or approved information becomes the final production reference; superseded information is not used for production preparation.

Pre-Production Approval Workflow

  1. Buyer sends schedules and drawings.
  2. VISFURN reviews manufacturing information and clarifies open technical points.
  3. Dimensions, finish and hardware references are confirmed against the latest approved information.
  4. Approved information is used for production preparation and opening labels follow the confirmed schedule.

Questions from Germany project buyers

What should a German project buyer include in a door schedule?

Include project type, opening ID, drawing reference, revision number, quantity, door leaf and opening dimensions, frame or jamb configuration, wall thickness, handing, hinge and lock positions, hardware and finish schedules, glazing where applicable, packing batch requirements and project documentation.

Which dimensions should be confirmed before production?

Confirm door leaf width and height, opening width and height, wall thickness, frame or jamb depth, handing and the latest approved drawing reference. Critical dimensions must be confirmed before production information is finalized.

How should drawing revisions be controlled during a door project?

The latest buyer-approved revision should be clearly identified by drawing number, schedule version, revision date and approval status. Superseded drawings or schedules should not remain the production reference.

What hinge and lock position information should be provided?

Provide hinge quantity where specified, hinge positions, lock or latch position, handle height where specified, closer or access-control preparation where applicable, handing, machining details and the relevant hardware schedule or drawing reference.

How should frame and jamb dimensions be coordinated with wall build-up?

Review frame type, jamb depth, wall build-up, architrave or trim where applicable, threshold, floor level, installation interface and the door or frame scope against the buyer-supplied project information.

How are finish references and samples confirmed before bulk production?

Identify the finish code, colour reference, laminate, veneer or painted finish where applicable, room or zone reference, sample approval and finish revision. Final appearance should be confirmed against approved samples and project finish references.

What project documentation should be reviewed for technical requirements?

Provide architectural drawings, door and opening schedules, frame details, wall build-up, hardware and finish schedules, project specification, required standards, authority or consultant requirements and revision records for manufacturing coordination and quotation.

How should opening IDs and packing labels match the approved schedule?

Use the approved schedule to identify opening ID, room or zone, door type, frame type, finish code, hardware group, packing batch and project phase. Labels should correspond to the approved schedule used for the package.
