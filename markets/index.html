Buyer questions

Project Door Market FAQs

Which countries does VISFURN provide project door quotations for?

VISFURN provides project door quotation paths for the United States, Canada, Australia, the United Kingdom, Germany, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates and Qatar through the market pages listed here.

What information should I send for a project door quotation?

Send the destination country, project type, quantity, door schedule or drawings, opening or door sizes, frame or jamb scope, finishes, hardware preparation, packing requirements and any project documentation that affects the requested door set.

Do door specifications change between project markets?

The required inputs can change by project market and project type. Existing openings, wall thickness, jamb depth, finish groups, hardware positions, documentation and packing identification should be reviewed against the buyer's own schedule and drawings.

Can I send a complete door schedule for multiple door types?

Yes. A complete schedule can include multiple door types, room uses, quantities, finish groups, frame or lining scope and hardware preparation. Clear opening or room identifiers help organize a mixed project package.

How are frame, hardware and finish requirements reviewed?

Frame or jamb scope, wall or opening dimensions, hinge and lock positions, hardware templates and finish information are reviewed with the project schedule and drawings before the requested door configuration is clarified.

Can project doors be identified by room or opening number?

Project doors can be planned around buyer-provided opening, room or phase identifiers so packing and package organization can be reviewed against the submitted project information.

What documents should be provided when a project has specific performance requirements?

Provide the relevant project specification, drawings, schedule, buyer-required documentation and any applicable project or authority requirements. Requirements are reviewed against the information supplied for that project rather than assumed.

How do I choose the most relevant market page?

Choose the market page that matches the project destination and read the procurement considerations most relevant to its project context, such as schedule coordination, existing openings, jamb scope, technical drawings, packing or approval workflow.
