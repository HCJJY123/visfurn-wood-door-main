Master-Slave Door System Customization

Customer: Stanley Odumenya, London, UK 63 IM Records | High-End Commercial Complex

Project Context

Stanley Odumenya, a London-based commercial real estate investor, was developing a premium commercial complex requiring professional bidirectional swing door systems. The project demanded both aesthetic excellence and functional reliability.

Core Customer Requirements

Design Specifications

  • Master door: Modern minimalist design with stainless steel accents
  • Slave door: Flat design, understated aesthetic
  • Material: Dark gray aluminum with premium brushed finish

Functional Requirements

  • Bidirectional automatic opening (interior and exterior)
  • Automatic closing (floor spring + top closer)
  • Optional 30% transparent tempered glass panels

Quality Standards

  • Aluminum hardness: 60-65 HB
  • Surface: 3-layer coating system (primer + intermediate + topcoat)
  • ISO 9001 certification required

Solution Development

Phase 1: Design Confirmation (Weeks 1-2)

3D rendering packages included:

  • Decorative stripe positioning and dimensions
  • Hardware installation methodology
  • Complete color and texture visualization

Customer consulted with their architects; approval received in writing.

Phase 2: Sample Fabrication (Weeks 3-4)

Two sample sets shipped to UK for on-site testing:

  • Operational smoothness: 100+ open-close cycles
  • Color verification under actual lighting
  • Installation complexity assessment

Samples received full approval; formal order placed.

Phase 3: Production (Weeks 5-8)

Rigorous quality control protocol:

  • 100% sampling inspection every 50-unit batch
  • Spectrophotometric color verification (ΔE<0.5)
  • Weekly progress reports with site photography

Phase 4: Delivery & Support (Week 9)

Component packaging for optimal logistics. Included:

  • Detailed installation manual
  • Video installation tutorial
  • 24-hour technical support

Project Outcomes

  • ✓ On-schedule delivery, zero delays
  • ✓ Zero quality defects, single-pass site acceptance
  • ✓ Order expansion: 100 units → 150 units
  • ✓ Customer referrals: 3 additional European projects sourced through this client

Success Factors

1. Design Communication Completeness

3D renderings and multi-round confirmations eliminated design interpretation discrepancies, preventing large-scale rework.

2. Standardized Sampling Process

Sending samples to actual use environments provided customers genuine product experience, increasing acceptance rates.

3. Production Transparency

Weekly progress reports with photography enabled customers to monitor project development and reduced delivery risk perception.

4. Exceptional Aftermarket Support

Installation manuals, video tutorials, and technical support facilitated customer project completion with confidence.

Market Impact

This single project generated three additional European opportunities through customer referral. The project demonstrates that premium quality combined with professional service creates lasting customer relationships and market expansion.
