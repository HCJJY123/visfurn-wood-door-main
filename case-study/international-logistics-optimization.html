International Logistics Optimization

Customer: Sarantuya Enkhbayar, Ulaanbaatar, Mongolia 6-Month Optimization Program | 59 IM Records

Background

Sarantuya Enkhbayar is a Mongolian furniture importer supplying hotels, office buildings, and commercial centers. His previous suppliers offered non-transparent pricing and unstable delivery schedules, causing project delays.

Sarantuya needed a supply partner offering clear cost structures, stable timelines, and consistent product quality.

Initial Challenges

1. Cost Structure Opacity

Previous suppliers quoted all-inclusive prices without breakdown. Sarantuya couldn't compare costs or budget effectively.

2. Multi-Batch Coordination Difficulty

Monthly purchases of 1-2 batches lacked economies of scale, driving up per-unit costs.

3. Delivery Instability

Tianjin-Ulaanbaatar routes offered variable transit times, making project planning difficult.

Solution: Logistics Optimization System

Step 1: Cost Transparency

We created a detailed cost breakdown template:

  • Product cost (unit price × quantity)
  • Domestic transport (factory → port)
  • Port operations (loading, documentation)
  • Ocean freight (based on container type)
  • Destination port clearing fees
  • Destination port pickup

Each line-item has transparent calculation methodology. Sarantuya now understands cost structure completely.

Step 2: Monthly Consolidated Purchasing

New operating model:

  • Mid-month: Customer submits all monthly requirements
  • Month-end: Consolidated shipment in single 20ft or 40ft container
  • Result: Superior ocean freight rates and lower per-unit costs

Cost Comparison:

  • Scattered LCL shipments: $350/cbm × average volume = High cost
  • Consolidated FCL: (Ocean freight ÷ total cbm) = 25-30% reduction

Step 3: Delivery Commitment

Standardized timeline guarantee:

  • Production: 10 working days post-order
  • Port loading: 2 working days after completion
  • Ocean transit: 25-30 days (fixed schedule)
  • Destination clearance: 3-5 working days
  • Total cycle: 40-45 days, locked in contract with penalty clause

6-Month Results

Financial Impact

  • 6-month cost savings: Approximately ¥180,000 (vs. previous supplier model)
  • Improved cash flow through predictable costs and timelines
  • Confidence to expand sales through reliable supply guarantees

Key Success Elements

1. Cost Transparency = Trust

When customers understand each cost component, they can budget confidently and build long-term relationships.

2. Consolidated Purchasing = Scale Economy

Benefits both supplier (stable capacity planning) and buyer (reduced unit costs).

3. Delivery Commitment > Price

Stable timelines often outweigh lower prices because they directly impact customer project success.

4. Regular Communication = Continuous Improvement

Monthly reviews with data feedback identify optimization opportunities systematically.
