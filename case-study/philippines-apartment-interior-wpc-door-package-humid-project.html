Project package guide

Apartment interior & WPC door packages

An apartment door package starts with a repeatable room schedule. Separate dry-room interior doors from bathroom and utility openings, then confirm dimensions, jamb depth, handing, finishes and the hardware included with each door mark.

This is an illustrative procurement guide for humid apartment projects, with a Philippines residential setting. It helps importers, developers and contractors prepare their own package; it is not a record of a delivered VISFURN condominium project.

Separate leaf-only prices from complete door sets

A low leaf-only price can omit the frame, architraves, hinges, lock, machining, packing or freight. Build a comparison by door mark and make the inclusions explicit. If the installer supplies some parts locally, record that responsibility in the schedule so the factory preparation matches the selected hardware.

For wooden, WPC and PVC door orders, the confirmed minimum is 50 sets in total and at least 10 sets per model. As a planning example, a 60-set schedule could contain 40 dry-room doors and 20 WPC bathroom doors across two models. This is an order-composition example, not a customer order or a commitment to a particular quotation.

Keep the four size references distinct

Record the rough opening, frame outer dimensions, door-leaf dimensions and required clear opening in separate fields. A 900 × 2100 mm leaf is not automatically a 900 × 2100 mm wall opening. The required opening depends on the selected frame, installation allowances, floor finish and assembly details.

Measure finished wall thickness after plaster, tile, wall lining and other finishes are considered. State jamb depth, trim coverage and any variation between room types. Add handing and swing direction to the drawing rather than relying only on a left/right label that different teams may interpret differently.

Control finishes and repeated room types

Use one door mark for each genuinely identical configuration. If a size, handing, frame depth, finish or hardware preparation differs, show the variation clearly rather than hiding it under the same room type. Keep a revision number on the approved schedule and finish board.

Review a representative sample before repeating the configuration across the building. Confirm the visible face, edges, frame finish, hardware positions and installation interface. If later phases need the same appearance, retain the approved reference and agree how the finish will be checked for subsequent orders.

Group the delivery by unit, floor or phase

Choose a label system that follows the installer's sequence. A packing list can group door marks by apartment type, floor or delivery phase, while identifying separate leaves, frames, trim and hardware. Confirm whether site teams will store full door sets together or receive components in different areas.

Share the destination port and receiving restrictions before the shipping plan is finalized. Use packed dimensions and the chosen protection to calculate volume. Mixed sizes, frame packages and accessories affect loading; a fixed number of leaves does not establish the container quantity or arrival date.

Buyer questions

Can interior and WPC doors be combined in one apartment order?

They can be reviewed together with separate models and quantities. For wooden, WPC and PVC door orders, the confirmed minimum is 50 sets in total and at least 10 sets per model.

Which dimensions should be sent for an apartment door quote?

Provide rough-opening width and height, leaf size if known, finished wall thickness, frame outer size if known, jamb depth, required clear opening and handing. Mark units explicitly.

How are repeated apartment doors kept organized?

Use distinct door marks, room or unit references, a controlled schedule revision and packing labels that match the agreed floor or phase sequence.

Is this evidence of a completed Philippines apartment project?

No. It is an illustrative apartment-door planning guide. The setting and images explain procurement decisions and are not verified VISFURN delivery references.
