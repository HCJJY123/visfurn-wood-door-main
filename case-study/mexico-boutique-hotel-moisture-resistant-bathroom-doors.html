Project package guide

Hotel guestroom & bathroom door packages

A hotel door package should separate guestroom entrances, bathroom doors and service openings. Specify each complete assembly, then coordinate the finish, frame depth, lock preparation and room labels before comparing supplier quotations.

Request a project quoteBrowse all project examples

This planning guide uses a Mexico boutique-hotel setting as an illustrative application. The approach is for importers, contractors and hotel procurement teams preparing a specification; it does not report a completed VISFURN hotel installation.

Define the full hotel door-set scope

A guestroom-door quotation is only comparable when its inclusions are clear. Identify whether the price covers the leaf alone, the leaf with frame and architraves, or the door set with specified hardware. List hinges, locks, closers, seals and thresholds separately, including who supplies each item.

Keep the visible finish reference with its approved sample. Specify whether the frame, architraves and leaf must match, and define the surface expected on the room side and corridor side. A catalogue image is useful for initial selection; the agreed sample and specification should govern the order.

Coordinate electronic locks before machining

Provide the lock model and the manufacturer's cutting template, door thickness, handing, hinge pattern and strike details. Include any access-control wiring or closer preparation that changes the door or frame. Confirm whether the hotel team supplies the locks or requires them in the quotation.

An electronic-lock drawing does not replace a complete opening review. Check the relationship between the lock, stile, frame stop, strike, seals and required clearances. Use an approved typical opening to resolve these interfaces before repeating the preparation across multiple rooms.

Approve one typical opening for each configuration

Group the schedule by room function and construction, not just by room number. A guestroom entrance and a bathroom door may need different leaves, frames and hardware even when the colour is similar. Define a separate approval reference for each configuration.

The sample review should cover appearance, dimensions, frame depth, trim joints, hardware interfaces and the agreed packing arrangement. If any specification changes after approval, record the new revision and the door marks affected. Agree production and shipping milestones after the configuration and quantity are confirmed.

Plan room labels and phased delivery

Use door marks and room numbers that correspond to the hotel schedule. Identify the leaf, frame, architraves and separate hardware packages so the site team can match them without opening unrelated cartons. Where room numbers may change, agree whether the permanent identifier should be the door mark instead.

For staged delivery, list the floors or zones in each shipment and the site's receiving sequence. Container planning depends on final packed dimensions, weights, pallet or crate requirements and the mix of door sizes. Confirm the destination port and the agreed freight scope; do not infer capacity from the room count alone.

Buyer questions

What is included in a hotel door package?

It depends on the agreed scope. Identify the leaf, frame, architraves, seals, hinges, locks, closers, machining and packing separately, then state which items are included and who supplies the rest.

Can WPC bathroom doors be matched with wooden guestroom doors?

The two constructions can be reviewed within the same project schedule. Confirm finishes, frame compatibility, exposure, hardware and samples separately for the bathroom and guestroom configurations.

What is needed for electronic hotel-lock preparation?

Send the exact lock model and cutting template, door thickness, handing, hinge pattern and strike details. Confirm the party supplying the locks and any wiring or access-control preparation.

Is this a completed project in Mexico?

No. It is an illustrative hotel-door procurement guide using a Mexico boutique-hotel setting. Images and planning examples are not verified customer delivery evidence.
