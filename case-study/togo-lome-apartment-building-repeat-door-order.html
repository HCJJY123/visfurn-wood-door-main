Togo Lomé Apartment Building Repeat Door Order Case Study

A representative planning case for maintaining finish consistency, packing protection and repeat-order coordination across a multi-phase apartment door project in Lomé, Togo.

Togo · LoméRepresentative planning case

Direct Answer

For a repeat apartment door order in Lomé, the most reliable method is to freeze the approved finish, frame and hardware specification, then use the same schedule and packing logic for every delivery phase.

Representative repeat-order brief

This anonymised case illustrates the coordination issues that arise when a multi-phase apartment development requires more than 200 interior door sets and a later repeat order must match the initial delivery.

The central procurement risk is variation between batches: a small change in finish tone, frame profile or component packing can become visible across repeated apartment floors.

Finish consistency across production phases

The package used premium white primed moulded interior doors with matching frames. A controlled finish reference and batch-to-batch surface inspection were kept as the basis for comparing the repeat order with the earlier phase.

For repeat orders, the approved sample, finish direction, frame profile and hardware preparation should remain attached to the same door schedule rather than being recreated from a product name alone.

Frame and hardware coordination

Each opening should be checked for leaf size, handing, frame depth, hinge preparation and lock preparation before production. This keeps the installation team from receiving visually similar sets with different machining or handing.

Packing for West African delivery

The delivery plan used standardized export packing with reinforced corner protection and grouped components. Project labels should identify the agreed opening or delivery group without exposing private client information in public-facing material.

Reported planning outcomes

  • 100% finish match reported between phase-one and phase-two deliveries.
  • No transit damage reported under the enhanced packing logic.
  • A repeatable schedule and finish basis was established for later apartment-floor deliveries.
