The challenge

Dark aluminium finishes can create very high surface temperatures in direct sun, while sandstorms place extra demand on perimeter seals. The conformity process also needed to be started before the container was booked.

The specification

The representative specification used cast aluminium leaves, thermal-isolation treatment, a light champagne powder coating, layered brush and EPDM seals, multipoint locks and steel security doors for secondary openings.

What changed

The finish moved from dark brown toward a lighter high-reflectance colour, a bottom sand brush was added, and the certification work was started five weeks before shipment so testing documents and the conformity route could progress together.

Logistics and results

The representative route ran from Xingang toward Dammam or Jeddah in approximately 20–28 days, subject to routing. Heavy aluminium doors may reach a weight limit before lightweight slabs reach a volume limit. The case records a pre-shipment conformity milestone and a first-summer performance target, not a universal guarantee.

FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask

Is SABER certification required before shipping to Saudi Arabia?

Confirm the current product scope and importer responsibility with the Saudi buyer, conformity provider and customs broker before shipment.

Do cast aluminium doors warp in 50°C heat?

Thermal movement depends on construction, finish, exposure, installation clearance and hardware; use a project-specific design rather than assuming every aluminium door behaves the same.

How many aluminium entry doors fit in a 40HQ?

The count depends on door weight, frame, packaging and the approved loading plan; heavy doors may be weight-limited.

What sealing do sandstorm regions need?

Layered perimeter seals, a suitable threshold and a bottom brush or drop seal can be reviewed for dusty conditions, subject to the actual opening and maintenance plan.

What fire standard applies in the GCC?

The required fire or life-safety standard depends on the building type and local approval; confirm the tested assembly with the project consultant.
