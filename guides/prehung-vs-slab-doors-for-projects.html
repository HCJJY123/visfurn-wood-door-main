Buyer decision guide

Prehung vs Slab Doors for Projects

The supply format changes the information required in the quotation and the work left for the site team.

Direct Answer

Choose prehung, slab or a complete door set according to who controls the frame, installation, hardware preparation and site tolerances.

Buyer decision table

FormatUsually suitsMust be confirmed
SlabProjects with local frame and installation controlLeaf size, handing, machining and site frame
PrehungRepeated openings with coordinated frame supplyJamb depth, rough opening, handing and hardware
Complete setBuyers wanting leaf, frame, trim and accessories mapped togetherAll components, packing and installation sequence

Check site responsibility

Confirm who measures the opening, supplies the frame, installs hinges and prepares the lock. The same leaf can require different information depending on whether the project buys a slab or a complete set.

Quotation input

Send the schedule, wall thickness, jamb depth, handing, frame and architrave requirements, hardware preparation and destination. This allows the supplier to identify the parts that are included and excluded.
