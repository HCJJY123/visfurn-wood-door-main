Galvanized steel school doors for high-traffic education buildings.

School doors take daily abuse from bags, trolleys, sports equipment and repeated corridor traffic. A galvanized steel substrate under powder coating helps protect the leaf when the finish is eventually scratched, especially around bottom rails, lock zones and corridor-facing surfaces.

Visfurn reviews school doors as project door sets: leaf, frame, hardware preparation, vision panel, safety glazing, edge-protection accessories, closer and finger-guard options are coordinated before quotation so each opening can match the education project schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why use galvanized steel instead of ordinary painted steel?

Galvanizing gives extra corrosion protection when the powder coat is scratched by daily school traffic, especially around bottom rails and high-contact areas.

Should classroom doors have vision panels?

Many education projects require visibility into occupied rooms. The panel size and location should be coordinated with safety glazing, privacy and lock security requirements.

Can finger guards be supplied?

Yes. Hinge-side finger guards can be planned for early-years and high-use areas, either factory prepared or supplied as part of the project hardware package.

What should buyers send for quotation?

Send opening schedule, room type, wall thickness, frame type, finish colour, vision panel requirement, hardware models, safety options, quantity and destination.
