What this page covers

A modern appearance coordinated as a complete opening.

The design reference may begin with a door image, but a quote-ready project needs the face pattern, finish, frame, wall condition, handing and hardware to be defined together.

Face pattern

Provide the number, direction, spacing and position of grooves or decorative inlays. A marked-up elevation is more reliable than a style name.

Finish control

Identify the colour or woodgrain direction and approve a physical or documented finish sample under the project workflow.

Door-set scope

Clarify leaf, frame, architrave, seals, hinge and lock preparation so all openings use the same approved design language.

Specification checklist

Confirm these inputs before quotation.

Opening data
Opening or leaf size, finished wall thickness and room/opening ID
Design reference
Dimensioned groove or inlay elevation, or a reference image for feasibility review
Finish
Colour, texture or woodgrain direction and the approval sample route
Frame scope
Frame profile and depth, architrave, threshold and adjacent wall finish
Hardware
Handing, hinges, lock body, handle, closer or other preparation where required
Exposure
Room type, humidity, cleaning, splash or direct-water condition
Commercial inputs
Quantity by type, packing labels, delivery phase and destination
Evidence
Any project performance or documentation requirement must be stated for review

Approval route

Move from a visual reference to a controlled bulk order.

1. Mark the elevation

Convert the preferred look into a clear drawing with pattern position, door handing and hardware locations.

2. Approve the sample

Check colour, grain direction, surface detail and the relationship between leaf, frame and architrave.

3. Release by schedule

Freeze type codes, quantities and labels before bulk production and export packing are planned.

Application references

Useful for repeated interior openings.

Buyer FAQ

Questions to settle before approval.

Can the groove pattern and finish be customized?

Groove or inlay layout and finish direction can be reviewed from a drawing or reference image. Final feasibility and appearance are confirmed through the project approval process.

Is this door suitable for every wet location?

No generic material label defines every exposure. Identify humidity, splash, cleaning and direct-water conditions so the full leaf, edge, frame, threshold and hardware configuration can be reviewed.

What should a buyer send for quotation?

Send the door schedule, leaf or opening sizes, finished wall thickness, groove reference, finish, frame and architrave scope, handing, hardware preparation, quantity and destination.
