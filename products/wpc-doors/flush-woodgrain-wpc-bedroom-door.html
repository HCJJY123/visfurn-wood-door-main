Buyer questions

Warm brown WPC bedroom door FAQ

Is this a grooved or raised-panel door?

No. This visual selection has a plain flush face with fine vertical woodgrain in a warm brown tone. The matching stepped casing and black lever are design references; confirm the final frame profile and hardware in the quotation.

What does the FOB quotation include?

FOB price: Request a project quotation. Port: tianjingang. Ask for a written scope separating the door leaf, frame, architrave, hardware, preparation and packing. No fixed amount or automatic hardware inclusion is stated.

Can the leaf and frame be made to project sizes?

Provide the door schedule with leaf or structural-opening dimensions clearly identified, finished wall thickness, handing and quantity. Feasible sizes, frame depth and machining require drawing and sample approval.

Does paint-free mean that no finishing approval is needed?

No. Paint-free describes the finished surface route, not a certification. Approve the colour, grain direction, sheen, edge appearance and matching trim on the proposed sample before production.

Is this product fire-rated or waterproof?

No fire, acoustic or waterproof rating is stated for this selection. Define the intended room exposure and required performance so product-specific documents and the complete assembly can be reviewed.

Do the images show completed projects?

No. The seven images are original design illustrations derived from the supplied visual references. They are not factory photographs, tested cross-sections or records of delivered projects.
