Buyer questions

Light woodgrain WPC door FAQ

What is included in the US$40–78 price range?

The reference price is US$40–78 per set including the door leaf and door frame, FOB Tianjin Port, China. Final price depends on the agreed configuration and quantity. Confirm trim, hardware, frame profile and packing in the written quotation; hardware inclusion is not assumed.

How does this design differ from the taupe woodgrain option?

This finish-led option has a lighter woodgrain appearance, a clean face and a fine inner border. It is a different visual direction within the WPC interior door range, not a claim of different core construction or performance.

Can I order the door in a specific size?

Send the door schedule with leaf or opening dimensions clearly identified, finished wall thickness, handing and quantity. VISFURN will confirm feasible dimensions, frame profiles and machining before the sample and order are approved.

Can this door be used in a bathroom or humid room?

Ask for a room-specific suitability review. Describe humidity, splash exposure and cleaning conditions, and confirm the leaf, frame, edge treatment and threshold together. This page does not claim a tested waterproof rating.

Is it suitable for a fire-rated hotel opening?

No fire or acoustic rating is stated for this design. If an opening requires tested performance, provide the standard and rating so a documented assembly can be reviewed separately.

Are these photographs of a delivered project?

The images are AI-generated design illustrations of the proposed light woodgrain finish, not factory photographs, test evidence or completed-project records. Approve the physical finish sample, drawings and supplied components before production.
