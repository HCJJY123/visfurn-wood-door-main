Buyer questions

Modern prehung WPC door FAQ

What does prehung mean for this WPC door?

A prehung package means the door leaf is hung in its specified frame before delivery. Confirm factory assembly, hinges, handing, frame depth and whether architraves, locks and handles are included. A loose leaf-and-frame kit is a different supply scope.

Is a WPC door the same as a solid wood door?

No. WPC means wood-plastic composite. It is a material category, not a guarantee of a solid core. The leaf construction, internal structure, thickness and surface finish must be confirmed for the quoted model.

Can this WPC interior door be used for a bathroom?

It can be reviewed for humid interior rooms. Tell us whether the opening sees humidity, wet cleaning, splashes or direct water. Suitability depends on the complete leaf, frame, edge treatment, threshold and hardware configuration.

Can I change the finish, size or horizontal line design?

Submit the required dimensions, finish reference and marked-up elevation for review. The illustrated oak tone and two horizontal inlays are a design direction; available profiles, construction and finish options are confirmed in the quotation and sample approval.

Is this a fire-rated hotel guestroom door?

No fire or acoustic rating is claimed for this model. Where a hotel opening needs tested performance, state the required standard and rating so a suitable documented assembly can be reviewed separately.

What are the price, minimum order and delivery time?

These depend on the approved construction, dimensions, finish, hardware, quantity and destination. Send the door schedule for a project quotation. Sample terms, minimum order, packing, trade terms and production timing are confirmed in that quotation.
