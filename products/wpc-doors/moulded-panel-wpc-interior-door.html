FAQ

Questions buyers should settle early.

Is this the same as the flush waterproof WPC door?

No. This page covers a moulded raised-panel WPC face. Compare it with the flush waterproof WPC interior door when the project needs a simpler modern profile.

Is the door termite-proof or fire-rated?

Do not treat the product name as proof of either claim. The structure may include a wood skeleton or chipboard filler. Ask for the exact supplier documentation and complete door-set evidence for the required project condition.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the opening schedule or drawings, leaf sizes, finished wall thickness, frame option, handing, finish, hardware preparation, quantity and destination port.
