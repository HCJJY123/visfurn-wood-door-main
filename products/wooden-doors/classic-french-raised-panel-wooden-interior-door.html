Panel profile & finish

Approve the details that make the design consistent.

Fix the panel elevation.

Provide a dimensioned face elevation with panel position, moulding widths, corner treatment and rail proportions. Resizing the leaf can change the visual balance, so review each door type in the schedule.

Approve the painted finish.

Agree the paint colour, sheen and surface standard using an identified sample. Review the leaf, frame and architrave together under the project approval process.

Coordinate hardware preparation.

Confirm the handle and lock models, backset, hinge locations and handing before machining. Distinguish hardware supplied with the door from buyer-supplied items.

Match the frame to the finished wall.

Record rough-opening dimensions, finished wall thickness and jamb depth. Confirm the frame profile, trim coverage, installation interface and whether the order is a loose kit or assembled door set.

For renovation work, include existing-frame measurements and photos. For repeated new-build openings, use door marks to keep sizes, finishes and packing groups aligned.

Buyer questions

French raised-panel wooden door FAQ

Is this a glazed French door or a double-door set?

This page shows a French-style single swing interior door with an opaque face and raised-panel detailing. French describes the design style here. Glazing, pairs or other layouts require a separate drawing and quotation.

Is the door made from solid timber?

The wooden-door category can include solid timber or engineered construction. This design does not establish the internal core or timber species. Confirm the leaf structure, face material, thickness and frame construction in the quotation.

Can the size, panel profile and paint colour be customized?

Send the leaf or rough-opening dimensions, desired panel elevation and paint reference. Visfurn reviews feasibility and coordinates the approved profile, finish sample, frame and hardware before production.

Can this door be used in a hotel?

It can be reviewed for hotel interior design schemes. No fire or acoustic rating is claimed for this design. For a guestroom entrance or other regulated opening, state the exact performance and documentation requirements for a suitable assembly review.

Is the painted wooden door suitable for a wet bathroom?

Do not assume suitability from the paint finish alone. State humidity, wet cleaning, splash or direct-water exposure. Leaf construction, edges, finish, frame and hardware must be reviewed together; a WPC route may also be considered.

How do I request a price, sample and delivery schedule?

Send quantities, dimensions, wall thickness, finish reference, supply scope, hardware and destination. Price, minimum order, sample terms, payment, packing and timing are confirmed in the project quotation.
