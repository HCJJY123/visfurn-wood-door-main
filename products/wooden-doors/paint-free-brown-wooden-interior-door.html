FAQ

Questions to settle before releasing production.

Can project documentation be reviewed?

Yes. Material documents and any required model-level test or certification records are reviewed against the selected door set and project requirement before approval.

What are the MOQ, sample and production terms?

MOQ is 5 sets. Typical production is 25 days after advance payment, technical documents and colour confirmation. The buyer pays sample fee and freight; a same-model 50-set bulk order can credit the sample fee, excluding freight, duties and special development costs.

Can the size and finish be customized?

Yes. Share the schedule, size, finish reference, frame scope and hardware preparation so the selected configuration can be reviewed before quotation.

What construction or performance values can be specified?

Core construction, leaf thickness and any project-required performance or certification documents are reviewed for the final configuration. They are not represented as fixed values on this page.

What should I send for a quote?

Send the door schedule or opening list, leaf sizes, finished wall thickness, frame and architrave scope, finish reference, hardware preparation, quantity and destination.
