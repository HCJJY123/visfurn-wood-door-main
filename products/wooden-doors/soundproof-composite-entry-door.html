Wooden doors / Project entrance door

Composite entry door: review the complete acoustic opening

An apartment or hotel corridor door may need acoustic separation, access control and a defined finish. Appearance alone cannot establish acoustic performance. The selected leaf, frame, seals, threshold, glazing, hardware and installation all need to be coordinated.

No acoustic rating, fire classification, certification, fixed lead time or price is claimed for this illustrated design. Request evidence for the exact quoted assembly when the project has a rated requirement.

What to confirm in the door schedule

  • Door marks, quantities by type and the intended room or corridor application.
  • Rough opening, proposed leaf dimensions, finished wall thickness and frame or jamb depth.
  • Door handing, swing clearance, lock, hinges, closer, seals, glazing and threshold scope.
  • Finish reference, architrave and packing labels for phased installation.
  • Required acoustic, fire or material documentation and the local approving authority.

Matching evidence to the ordered assembly

If a project specifies a laboratory acoustic rating, request a report identifying the complete assembly and its tested size, seals and installation conditions. A leaf-only test or a different frame cannot be assumed to establish the performance of the proposed doorset. Fire and smoke requirements need separate evidence for the complete rated configuration.

Review the standards and documentation checklist, then send drawings and the destination port through the existing project inquiry form. Standard bulk orders start at 50 sets overall and 10 sets per model; confirm any project exception in the quotation.
