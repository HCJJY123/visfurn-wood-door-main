Buyer Answer

What should buyers confirm before ordering entrance doors?

Entrance doors should be specified by opening size, wall thickness, frame depth, security hardware, weather exposure and finish direction. For villa and apartment projects, confirm handing, lock preparation and packing labels before approving bulk production.

Quote-ready details

  • Opening size, wall thickness and handing
  • Frame depth, threshold and security hardware
  • Finish sample, weather exposure and quantity
  • Destination port, packing marks and delivery schedule

Entrance Door Buying FAQ

What details are needed for a front-entry quotation?

Send the opening size, wall thickness, handing, finish reference, hardware list, quantity and destination port so the front-entry package can be reviewed correctly.

Can villa entry doors be customized?

Yes. Door leaf structure, frame depth, decorative direction and hardware preparation can be reviewed against the project drawings and sample approval process.

What should be confirmed before production?

Confirm frame depth, security level, weather exposure, finish specification and packing requirements before production is released.
