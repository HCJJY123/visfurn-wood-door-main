Buyer Answer

What makes hotel doors different from standard interior doors?

Hotel guestroom doors usually require acoustic performance, electronic lock preparation, closer reinforcement, viewer position, consistent veneer or laminate finish, and room-by-room packing labels. These details should be confirmed before sample approval and mass production.

Hotel quote checklist

  • Room schedule, door marks and opening sizes
  • Acoustic target and fire rating if required
  • Electronic lock, closer, hinge and viewer drawings
  • For renovation, existing frame depth, leaf thickness and hinge or lock locations
  • Finish sample, frame details and installation sequence
  • Review the hotel door package checklist

Quick answer

Hotel door packages are quote-ready when each opening is tied to its room type, frame condition, finish, hardware and delivery phase.

For a hotel renovation or new-build package, the supplier needs an opening schedule first, then a controlled review of acoustic or fire requirements, electronic-lock preparation, existing-frame compatibility and room-by-room packing sequence.

Hotel Door Buying FAQ

What should be confirmed for a hotel guestroom door package?

Confirm room schedule, opening size, wall thickness, frame type, acoustic requirement, fire rating if needed, electronic lock model, closer, viewer, finish sample and packing labels.

Can hotel doors be prepared for electronic locks?

Yes. Lock case mortice, cylinder bore, latch face, viewer hole and closer reinforcement can be prepared before finishing when hardware drawings are provided.

How is finish consistency controlled?

Finish material can be reserved by batch where possible, approved samples are retained, and leaves, frames and architraves are reviewed against the same room schedule.

Can VISFURN quote replacement doors for existing hotel frames and electronic locks?

Yes, subject to an opening-by-opening review. Share clear opening measurements, frame depth, leaf thickness, handing, hinge and lock details, plus photos or a sample confirmation where needed before finalizing the quotation.

Hotel Door Package Buyer Questions

What should be separated in a hotel door schedule?

Separate guestroom, bathroom, corridor, service-area and back-of-house openings. Each group may need different fire, acoustic, moisture and hardware requirements.

How should hotel acoustic doors be specified?

Define the target acoustic rating, door core, perimeter seal, threshold condition and wall construction. A door leaf alone cannot guarantee site acoustic performance.

Why do hotel buyers need sample approval?

Sample approval locks finish color, frame profile, edge detail and hardware preparation before mass production, reducing variation across repeated rooms.

What helps prevent installation mix-ups?

Use packing labels by floor, room type, room number and opening ID so installers can match the correct door set to each opening after delivery.
