Buyer questions

Before you specify this door

Is this a steel door or a wooden door?

The references establish the appearance only. Leaf material, core, frame construction and thickness must be confirmed in the project specification; no material is inferred from the images.

Are both proportions available in the same configuration?

The single-door and wider elevations are design references. Confirm the final dimensions, number of operable leaves, handing and frame layout on an approved drawing.

Can it be used in a fully exposed entrance?

Suitability must be reviewed against rain, sun, coastal conditions and the required performance standard. Confirm the construction, finish, threshold and seals before approving an exposed location.

Does the pictured hardware include a smart lock?

The image shows a hardware appearance, not confirmed lock functions. Specify the lock model, cylinder, access-control needs and whether hardware is supplied or preparation only.

What is the price and delivery time?

Request a project quotation with quantities, opening sizes, configuration, hardware scope and destination. Price, minimum order quantity and lead time are confirmed with the quotation.

Are the images photographs of delivered doors?

No. These are AI-generated design visualizations based on the supplied appearance references. They are not factory photographs, installation records or evidence of tested performance.
