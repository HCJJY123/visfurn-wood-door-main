Frequently Asked Questions

Q: What is the lead time for hospital door orders?

A: For standard sizes and colors in stock, delivery is 7-10 business days. Custom orders typically require 15-25 days depending on specifications and quantity. Rush orders can be accommodated - please contact us for details.

Q: Are these doors FDA compliant?

A: Yes, all our hospital doors are FDA compliant and meet medical facility hygiene standards. We can provide certification documents and test reports upon request.

Q: Can the doors be customized to specific sizes?

A: Absolutely. We offer full customization including door size, window size, color, hardware options, and additional features like access control systems. Send us your requirements for a detailed quote.

Q: What is the fire rating of these doors?

A: Our hospital doors have a 90-minute fire rating certified to BS 476 and EN 1634 standards. Fire test reports are available for verification.

Q: Do you provide installation services?

A: We provide comprehensive installation guidance and technical drawings. For international orders, we can recommend qualified local installers. Installation videos and manuals are included with every order.

Q: What warranty do you offer?

A: We offer a 3-year warranty covering manufacturing defects. This includes the door frame, panel, hardware, and finish. Normal wear and tear is not covered.

Q: What payment methods do you accept?

A: We accept T/T (bank transfer), L/C (Letter of Credit), and PayPal. Standard terms are 30% deposit with order confirmation, 70% balance before shipment. Other payment arrangements can be discussed for large orders.

Q: How are the doors packaged for shipping?

A: Each door is carefully protected with foam padding, wrapped in carton, and packed in wooden crates meeting international export standards. This ensures safe delivery even for long-distance shipping.
