Detailed Product Description

The Visfurn Premium Hospital Door represents the pinnacle of medical facility door engineering, combining rigorous safety standards with practical functionality for demanding healthcare environments. Designed specifically for hospitals, surgical centers, laboratories, and cleanroom facilities, this door system delivers uncompromising performance in fire safety, hygiene, and durability.

Superior Construction

At the heart of our hospital door is a 45mm thick fire-rated solid core that provides 90 minutes of fire resistance, exceeding most building code requirements. The core is encased in premium High-Pressure Laminate (HPL) surfaces that are not only aesthetically pleasing but also highly resistant to impact, scratches, chemicals, and moisture. The HPL surface features an antibacterial coating that inhibits bacterial growth, making it ideal for sterile medical environments.

Observation Window

The integrated tempered glass observation window (600mm x 400mm) allows for safe visual monitoring without compromising the door's structural integrity or fire rating. The window is secured with an aluminum frame and meets safety glass standards for impact resistance. This feature is particularly valuable in operating rooms, intensive care units, and patient rooms where visual oversight is critical.

Professional Hardware

Each door comes equipped with heavy-duty ball-bearing hinges rated for high-frequency use, ensuring smooth operation even after millions of cycles. The stainless steel kickplate (400mm height) protects the lower portion of the door from wheelchair and cart impacts, while the concealed door closer ensures controlled, quiet closure that complies with ADA requirements.

Installation Guidelines

Professional installation is recommended to ensure optimal performance and warranty validation. The following guidelines provide an overview of the installation process:

Pre-Installation Requirements

  • Opening Preparation: Ensure door opening is square, plumb, and level. Rough opening should be 20mm larger than door frame on all sides.
  • Wall Strength: Wall structure must support door weight (45-55kg). Reinforce with additional studs if necessary.
  • Clearances: Verify adequate clearance for door swing (minimum 90° opening required).
  • Hardware: Confirm all hardware components are present before beginning installation.

Installation Steps

  1. Frame Installation: Set door frame in opening using shims to ensure plumb and level. Secure frame with appropriate fasteners at 300mm intervals.
  2. Hinge Mounting: Attach hinges to door and frame. Ensure proper alignment and spacing (typically 200mm from top/bottom, third hinge centered).
  3. Door Hanging: Carefully hang door on hinges. Check for proper clearance and smooth swing operation.
  4. Hardware Installation: Install lock, handle, and door closer according to manufacturer specifications.
  5. Sealing: Apply weather stripping and door bottom seal for sound and smoke containment.
  6. Adjustment: Fine-tune hinge positions and door closer tension for optimal operation.
  7. Final Inspection: Verify door closes properly, latches securely, and meets all clearance requirements.

Maintenance Recommendations

  • Clean surface with mild detergent and soft cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners.
  • Lubricate hinges and closer annually with appropriate lubricant.
  • Inspect and tighten hardware fasteners every 6 months.
  • Check door alignment and adjust as needed to prevent binding.
  • Replace weatherstripping when worn or damaged.
⚠️ Important: Installation must comply with local building codes and fire safety regulations. Professional installation by licensed contractors is strongly recommended to maintain warranty validity.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the fire rating of these doors?
Our hospital doors have a 90-minute fire rating, tested and certified according to BS 476: Part 22 and UL 10C standards. This exceeds most building code requirements for healthcare facilities.
Are these doors suitable for cleanroom environments?
Yes, the smooth HPL surface with sealed edges prevents particle accumulation and is easy to sanitize, making these doors ideal for cleanroom applications. They can be specified with additional sealing for pressure control if needed.
Can I order custom sizes?
Absolutely. We offer custom sizing for widths ranging from 700mm to 1200mm and heights from 1800mm to 2400mm. Custom orders typically require 3-4 weeks lead time. Contact our sales team for a quote.
What cleaning products are safe to use?
The HPL surface is resistant to common hospital disinfectants including quaternary ammonium compounds, alcohol-based cleaners, and bleach solutions (up to 10% concentration). Avoid abrasive cleaners or scouring pads. Always follow your facility's cleaning protocols.
Is professional installation required?
While not strictly required, professional installation by licensed contractors is strongly recommended to ensure proper operation, fire rating integrity, and warranty validity. Improper installation may void the warranty.
What hardware is included?
Standard package includes heavy-duty ball-bearing hinges and stainless steel kickplate. Door closers, locks, handles, and other hardware can be added based on your specifications. We offer complete hardware packages for convenience.
Can the observation window be customized?
Yes, we can customize window size, position, and glass type (clear, frosted, or wire-reinforced) based on your requirements. Minimum order quantities may apply for significant customizations.
What is the warranty coverage?
We provide a 5-year manufacturer warranty covering defects in materials and workmanship. This includes warping, delamination, hardware failure, and finish degradation under normal use. Warranty is void if doors are improperly installed or maintained.
Do you offer bulk discounts for large projects?
Yes, we offer competitive pricing for bulk orders. Projects requiring 20+ doors qualify for volume discounts. Contact our sales team with your project details for a customized quote.
How do I maintain the antibacterial properties?
The antibacterial coating is integrated into the HPL surface and does not require special maintenance. Regular cleaning with standard hospital disinfectants will not diminish its effectiveness. The coating remains active for the life of the door.
