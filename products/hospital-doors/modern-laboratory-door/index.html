Is a paired swing door right for this opening?

A double-leaf swing arrangement can be considered when the room plan calls for a paired hinged opening. It is not a substitute for checking the usable passage width, leaf swing, traffic route, nearby equipment and clearance on both sides. Identify which leaf is active in everyday use and how the other leaf is secured or released. If the opening cannot accommodate a swing path, review the separate automatic sliding door for clinical openings as a different design option; do not transfer any sealing or performance claim between these products.

Use the project team's room data sheet and door schedule to decide suitability. A laboratory, patient-support room and controlled clinical space can have different access, observation and cleaning requirements even when their door appearance is similar.

Surface, glazing and cleaning review

Ask for the proposed finish sample and written cleaning guidance for the specified surface, edge and frame. The visible door appearance does not establish chemical resistance, antimicrobial performance or suitability for a particular infection-control protocol. List the cleaning agents and cleaning frequency required by the facility so the project team can assess the proposed materials and maintenance instructions.

The vision panel should be coordinated with staff sightlines, occupant privacy and the relevant glazing schedule. Confirm glass type, placement and any shielding or privacy requirement in the project drawings. A photographed pane is not evidence that a particular glass, fire rating or privacy treatment is included in the quoted set.

Fire, smoke and other performance requirements

If the opening is designated as fire- or smoke-rated, request the applicable evidence for the complete proposed assembly: leaf, frame, glazing, seals, hardware, rating, permitted dimensions and installation details. The project designer and authority having jurisdiction should confirm which rules apply at the destination. Do not assume that a rating for one component, door size or test configuration extends to this opening.

The same discipline applies to acoustic, hygiene, radiation-shielding or controlled-environment requirements: state the project criterion and ask for documentation that matches the exact proposed configuration. If the evidence is not available, keep the requirement open rather than treating it as supplied.

Agree the supply boundary before comparing prices

Ask each bidder to identify whether the offer includes only the leaves or also frames, glazing, hardware, closers, protection plates, seals, factory preparation and packing labels. Clarify which components are customer-supplied and who approves their machining templates. Review any listed price range against the written scope; the range alone does not establish the final door-set specification or project compliance.

For a comparable proposal, send the opening schedule, drawings, hardware and glazing schedules, finish reference, performance requirements, quantities and destination. Frequently asked buyer questions

Is this the automatic hermetic sliding door?

No. This page discusses a paired hinged laboratory-door configuration. The automatic sliding door is a separate product and requires its own opening and system review.

Can a fire rating be assumed from the page image or the leaf material?

No. A required rating must be supported by documentation applicable to the complete proposed assembly, its dimensions, glazing, hardware and installation conditions.

What should we send for a quotation with vision panels?

Provide the room schedule, opening and clear-passage requirements, panel position, glazing and privacy requirements, frame and wall details, hardware set, quantity and destination. State any required performance documentation separately.

Can we reuse existing frames or buyer-supplied hardware?

That must be evaluated for each opening. Send measured frame details, condition photos and the approved hardware templates; identify what VISFURN is expected to supply or prepare. Do not assume a replacement leaf will fit an existing frame without review.

Does the displayed price include a rated assembly or all hardware?

The published range does not, by itself, define the supply scope or any tested performance. Request a written, opening-specific quotation showing the included parts and documentation.
