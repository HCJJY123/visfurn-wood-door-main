Automatic hermetic sliding door set for clinical controlled openings.

This hospital door system combines a sealed sliding leaf, operator unit, track, sensor activation and project-specific frame interface. It is used where swing-door clearance is limited or where operating rooms and clean areas need smooth access with reduced air leakage around the leaf.

For procurement, the important point is not only the panel appearance. Clear opening, wall thickness, track support, power location, interlock logic, radiation shielding and maintenance access should be reviewed before the opening is closed on site.

Options & Configurations

Each hospital opening should be quoted as a configured set. Visfurn can review the following options according to drawings and room requirements.

  • Vision panel, observation window or privacy glass layout.
  • Lead lining for X-ray or imaging rooms, supplied according to specified lead equivalence.
  • Touchless activation, foot sensor, hand switch, access control and interlock logic.
  • Single-leaf, bi-parting or telescopic sliding configurations where the opening allows.
  • Frame, wall trim and clean edge detailing coordinated with wall finish.

Applications

Typical uses include operating rooms, ICU areas, clean corridors, isolation suites, preparation rooms, imaging support rooms and healthcare areas where automatic sealed access is required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the automatic hermetic door priced per opening?

Yes. Size, operator, lead lining, access control, interlock logic and wall condition can change the cost, so each opening should be quoted from drawings or a door schedule.

Can it be used for operating rooms?

Yes, it is commonly specified for operating rooms and clean healthcare areas where a sealed sliding door set and smooth activation are required.

Can lead lining be added?

Lead lining can be reviewed when the required lead equivalence is provided. The leaf, frame overlap and operator capacity must be configured together.

What information is needed before production?

Provide clear opening, wall thickness, room type, operator and activation requirements, lead lining need, interlock or access control requirements, quantity and destination.
