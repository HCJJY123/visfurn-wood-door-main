Buyer answer: what to confirm before ordering

For a project quotation, send the door schedule and the approval requirement together. This lets the factory review the complete door set instead of pricing an unsuitable leaf-only configuration.

  • Opening size, wall thickness, handing and frame installation condition.
  • Required fire-resistance or security standard, destination market and approving authority.
  • Lock, closer, hinge, finish and threshold requirements, plus the project quantity.
  • Target delivery date, destination port and export packing requirements.

Certification Guidance

Fire resistance, smoke control, acoustic performance and security resistance are not interchangeable claims. A rating accepted in one market may not be accepted by another project authority. Share the destination country, building type and approval requirement before pricing, and we will quote the construction that can be supplied against the correct certificate package.

  • United States projects may require listed assemblies accepted by the local authority having jurisdiction.
  • European projects usually review EN 1634-1 / local classification documents by full assembly.
  • Other markets may require national standards, project consultant approval or third-party inspection.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you supply certified fire-rated steel doors?

Yes, but the exact build must be selected against the project certificate requirement. Fire-resistance builds are quoted after confirming destination market, approving authority, opening schedule and hardware.

Are fire resistance and security rating the same thing?

No. Fire resistance and burglary resistance are separate test systems. They should be reviewed independently so the project team does not rely on the wrong document.

Why is the frame supplied with the door leaf?

Fire and security performance depends on the full assembly, not only the slab. The steel frame, seal, hinge, lock preparation and installation condition all affect approval and site performance.

Can finishes and dimensions be customized?

Custom size, colour, woodgrain transfer finish, frame depth and hardware preparation can be reviewed against the selected certified build and batch quantity.

What information should buyers send first?

Send the door schedule, wall thickness, handing, lock and closer requirements, required standard or authority, quantity, destination and any consultant specification.
