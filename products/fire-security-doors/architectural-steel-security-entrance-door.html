Buyer questions

Steel entrance door FAQ

What is the FOB price?

Request a project quotation. The FOB port is Tianjingang (Tianjin Port), China. Price and included components are confirmed against quantities, drawings, finish, hardware and packing requirements.

Does this page establish a fire or burglary-resistance rating?

No. No fire duration, smoke rating, burglary-resistance class or certification is claimed for these design options. Submit the required standard and full opening configuration for project-specific documentation review before approval.

Are the listed dimensions a confirmed stock specification?

No. The figures are reference configuration options from the sourcing brief, not stock sizes or a promise that every combination is available. Confirm leaf size versus structural opening, frame depth, steel gauge and hardware against the approved quotation and drawings.

Can the design be used at an exposed outdoor entrance?

The actual exposure must be reviewed first. Send rain, sun and coastal conditions, the canopy or recess details and the proposed threshold and seals. A steel surface or a product image alone does not establish exterior weather performance.

What do the catalogue images represent?

The images show distinct catalogue design references, not one SKU, delivered customer projects, test evidence or a record of internal construction. Select a design for each opening and approve its physical sample, component schedule and drawings before production.
