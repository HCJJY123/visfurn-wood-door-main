Practical buying answer

Is a PVC-faced wooden door the same as a WPC door?

No. PVC facing describes a surface finish; WPC describes a wood-plastic composite material. A quotation must identify the door-leaf construction and the frame separately. A woodgrain finish or similar photograph does not make two door sets equivalent.

Compare the door components separately

Door leafCore or body construction, facing, thickness and edge detail.A finish name alone does not define the complete leaf.

WPC Door Buying FAQ

Where are WPC doors most suitable?

WPC doors can be considered for interior bedrooms, bathrooms and utility rooms, but the material name does not establish waterproof, fire or acoustic performance. Confirm the selected leaf, frame, edges, hardware and documented limits for the actual room exposure.

Can WPC doors be supplied with matching frames?

Yes. Matching WPC frames and architraves can be reviewed with the door leaf finish so the complete opening is coordinated before production.

What details are needed for quotation?

Send leaf size, wall thickness, frame requirement, finish colour, lock and hinge preparation, quantity, room application and destination port.

What wet-area details should be included before quotation?

State the room type and moisture exposure, then provide leaf and frame dimensions, wall thickness, threshold or bottom-clearance detail, hardware environment, finish, quantity and packing requirements.
