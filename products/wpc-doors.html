Buyer Answer

When should a project buyer choose WPC doors?

Consider WPC doors for bathrooms, utility rooms, student housing, apartments, hotels and other moisture-prone project areas. Confirm the expected exposure, frame material, wall thickness, edge treatment and hardware machining before bulk production.

Quote-ready details

  • Room type and humidity exposure: humidity only, regular wet cleaning, splash exposure or direct water
  • Leaf size, frame profile, wall thickness and tile-to-frame detail
  • Threshold, drainage direction or bottom-clearance requirement
  • Surface color, texture and matching architrave
  • Lock, hinge, handing, hardware environment and packing label requirements
  • Compare WPC and PVC laminated doors for humid areas

Quick answer

WPC bathroom door packages are quote-ready when the wet-area opening, frame depth, threshold, hardware and tile transition are confirmed as one system.

WPC selection is only the starting point. A practical quote needs the finished wall condition, door size and handing, frame option, hinge and lock preparation, threshold detail and the intended room-by-room packing logic.

WPC Door Buying FAQ

Where are WPC doors most suitable?

WPC doors are suitable for bathrooms, utility rooms, hotels, apartments and humid climate projects where timber or MDF doors may swell from moisture exposure.

Can WPC doors be supplied with matching frames?

Yes. Matching WPC frames and architraves can be reviewed with the door leaf finish so the complete opening is coordinated before production.

What details are needed for quotation?

Send leaf size, wall thickness, frame requirement, finish colour, lock and hinge preparation, quantity, room application and destination port.

What wet-area details should be included before quotation?

State the room type and moisture exposure, then provide leaf and frame dimensions, wall thickness, threshold or bottom-clearance detail, hardware environment, finish, quantity and packing requirements.
