Frequently asked questions for door procurement.

WPC (Wood-Plastic Composite) doors are 100% waterproof and termite-proof, ensuring zero maintenance and long-term durability in humid climates, which is essential for managing high-volume B2B project budgets.

Professional hotel doors should comply with UL 10B/10C or BS 476 fire ratings and achieve an STC (Sound Transmission Class) rating of 35 or higher to ensure guest safety and room-to-room acoustic privacy for 4-star and 5-star hospitality developments.

Read the door-size guide

The rough opening is the opening prepared in the wall for installation, while the door leaf size refers only to the movable door panel. The frame occupies part of the opening, so rough opening, frame outer dimensions and door leaf dimensions should not be treated as the same measurement.

Review size terminology

Finished wall thickness affects the required frame depth and the way the frame, architrave or trim covers the wall. If frame depth does not match the completed wall build-up, installation and final appearance may be affected.

Wooden doors and WPC doors differ in material composition, surface options, moisture behavior, repair characteristics and application requirements. The correct choice depends on the installation environment, finish requirements, budget and complete project specification.

Export packing should protect the door leaf, frame, finish, corners and hardware from handling damage. The final packing method depends on the door type, project quantity and shipment arrangement.
