Custom Interior Wood Doors for Multi-Door Projects

Use this page when a project needs repeated openings, mixed sizes or a complete door-set specification rather than a one-door retail purchase.

Direct Answer

VISFURN helps B2B buyers coordinate custom interior wood door leaves, frames, jambs, architraves and hardware preparation from one project schedule.

What the project review covers

A useful quotation starts with the opening schedule and the complete set around each leaf. Share custom door sizes, rough opening versus leaf size, finished wall thickness, jamb depth, frame and architrave requirements, handing, finish, glass and hardware preparation.

Material options can include wooden, engineered, MDF or veneer constructions, with paint, veneer, PVC or melamine finishes where supported by the selected product specification.

Specification points buyers should freeze

DecisionInformation to confirm
SizeRough opening, leaf size, frame size and finished wall thickness
Door setSlab, prehung or complete set with frame and architrave
FinishPaint, veneer, PVC or melamine reference and approved sample
OperationHanding, opening direction, glass and hardware preparation
DeliveryDestination, packing sequence, labels and export documentation

What to send for a project quotation

  1. Door schedule or opening list with marks and quantities.
  2. Rough opening and leaf dimensions, including units.
  3. Finished wall thickness and jamb depth where known.
  4. Frame, architrave, handing, glass and hardware requirements.
  5. Finish references, drawings, photos and destination port.

Without drawings, VISFURN can review the stated configuration but cannot confirm installation fit, local code acceptance or final hardware compatibility.
