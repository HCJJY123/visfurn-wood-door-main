Custom Interior Wood Doors for US Projects

For US multifamily, residential, hospitality and commercial projects, send the door schedule before assuming a listed size is stock.

Direct Answer

VISFURN reviews US interior wood door projects from an imperial schedule, matching custom leaf sizes with rough openings, jambs, frames, architraves and hardware preparation.

Imperial sizes in a project schedule

Common references may include 28x80, 30x80, 32x80, 34x80, 36x80, 40x82, 42x82, 48x80 and 8 ft doors. These are schedule references, not a statement that every size is standard stock. Confirm each opening, unit convention and frame configuration before production.

Rough opening, jamb and complete set

The rough opening is not the same as the door leaf. A quotation should distinguish the opening, leaf, frame and finished wall thickness. Where appropriate, 6.5-inch or 7-inch jamb concepts can be reviewed against the wall build-up. Choose slab, prehung or full-set supply based on the installation responsibility and the project schedule.
