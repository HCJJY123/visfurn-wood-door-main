PVC Laminated Wooden Doors vs Painted Wooden Doors: Which Is Better for Projects?

A practical B2B comparison for buyers choosing interior door finishes for apartments, hotels, bedrooms, offices and humid-area projects.

Apartments / Hotels / Interior projectsPrepared for B2B door buyers

Direct Answer

PVC laminated wooden doors are usually better when buyers need consistent wood grain, easier cleaning and improved moisture resistance. Painted wooden doors are better when the project requires a specific paint color, repainting flexibility or a more traditional finish specification.

Start with the room schedule

Do not choose the finish only by appearance. Bedrooms, apartment corridors, hotel guest rooms, offices and utility areas face different cleaning routines, humidity exposure and traffic levels.

For repeat-unit projects, the supplier should review door leaf size, frame, architrave, color board, hardware preparation and packing labels together.

Where PVC laminated doors perform well

PVC laminated wooden doors use a decorative film over an MDF or engineered wooden structure. The surface can provide stable wood grain, consistent color across batches and easier cleaning than many painted surfaces.

  • Apartment bedrooms and rental units
  • Hotel guestroom interior doors
  • Office and commercial interiors
  • Projects requiring repeatable finish consistency

Where painted wooden doors still make sense

Painted wooden doors are useful when the designer needs a precise paint system, repainting flexibility or a solid-color finish that matches other architectural elements. However, buyers should confirm primer, topcoat, edge sealing and packing protection before mass production.

Moisture and cleaning considerations

Bathrooms, utility rooms and humid corridors need closer review. PVC laminated surfaces may handle daily cleaning and humidity better, but edge sealing, frame material and ventilation still matter. For higher moisture exposure, WPC doors may be more suitable than either PVC laminated or painted wooden doors.

RFQ checklist for buyers

  • Room type and quantity by opening
  • Door leaf size, thickness and core option
  • PVC film code or paint color reference
  • Frame width, wall thickness and architrave profile
  • Hardware preparation and handing
  • Moisture exposure and cleaning routine
  • Sample approval before mass production
