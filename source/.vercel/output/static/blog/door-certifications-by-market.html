Door Certifications by Market: US, EU, UK, Australia and the Gulf — What Your Shipment Actually Needs

A destination-by-destination map for door buyers checking documents, labels and test reports before shipment.

GuideCompliance

Quick answer

Door certification requirements are market-specific. The US commonly requires TSCA Title VI certification and labelling for composite wood doors plus Lacey Act declaration. The EU uses CE marking and Declaration of Performance for relevant door sets. Australia requires AS 1905.1 for fire-rated door assemblies, while Saudi Arabia and GCC shipments may require SABER / SASO conformity documentation before shipment.

What is a Declaration of Performance?

A Declaration of Performance is issued by the manufacturer for products covered by a harmonised standard and supported by test or notified body documentation. It should match the actual door construction, size range and intended use.

Do internal doors need CE marking in Europe?

The answer depends on product type, market and whether the item is supplied as a doorset. Buyers should not assume that a slab, frame package and fire-rated doorset share the same documentation route.

Why do Saudi shipments get stuck at the port?

Saudi shipments can be delayed when SABER / SASO conformity steps are started after goods arrive. Importers should confirm the HS code, product scope, test reports and certificate process before shipment.

Which certificates can a Chinese factory provide?

A factory can provide its business documents, ISO certificates, product test reports, technical datasheets, labels, packing details and manufacturer declarations where applicable. Some documents, such as importer account applications or local import approvals, must be handled by the buyer or local agent.

How much does testing cost and how long does it take?

Fire, acoustic and formaldehyde testing can take several weeks depending on laboratory schedule, sample preparation and report scope. If a project needs a specific standard, build the testing timeline into the quotation stage.
