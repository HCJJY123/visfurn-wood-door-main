How to send door sizes for a fast quotation

Direct Answer

A fast quotation starts with a clear door schedule. Send the opening width and height, wall thickness, door leaf thickness, opening direction, frame type, hardware, finish, quantity and destination port.

Key takeaways

  • Measure the finished opening width and height in millimeters.
  • Confirm wall thickness because it affects the door frame and architrave.
  • Mark the opening direction for every room or door number.
  • List hardware requirements such as lock, hinge, handle, stopper and closer.
  • Group identical sizes together so quantity and packing can be calculated faster.

What to send Visfurn

Send door type, width, height, wall thickness, frame style, hardware list, color or finish, quantity and destination port. If available, include project drawings or a door schedule.
