The challenge

A remote resort site needed doors and hardware that could tolerate humid conditions, salt air and limited replacement access while maintaining a high-end hospitality appearance.

The specification

The representative package combined timber-finish guestroom doors, WPC bathroom doors, moisture-conscious frames and stainless hardware. Fire and acoustic requirements must be confirmed per room type.

What changed

Hardware was upgraded for the marine environment, bathroom doors were separated from dry-area door specifications and a spare-parts allowance travelled with the main shipment.

Logistics and results

The route was planned from Xingang through Colombo to Malé in approximately 22–28 days. The case records a first-rainy-season performance target and fewer individual replacement shipments, subject to site maintenance.
