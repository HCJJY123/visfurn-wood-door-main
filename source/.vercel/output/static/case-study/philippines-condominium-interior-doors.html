Key constraints

The challenge

The project needed doors that could handle fast rental turnover and tropical humidity without the budget of an all-solid-wood specification.

The specification

Bedrooms and living areas used scratch-resistant melamine-faced doors, bathrooms used WPC doors, and the representative schedule standardised openings at 800 × 2100mm.

What changed

Three shipments were phased around handover dates, one size and one hardware family reduced the SKU count from nine to two, and illustrated installation instructions travelled with the order.

Logistics and results

The representative route was planned from Xingang to Manila South Harbor in approximately 10–16 days. The summary records on-time phased delivery, an estimated 7% quotation reduction from SKU simplification and below 1% door-related rework at handover.

FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask

Are melamine doors durable enough for rental apartments?

They can be suitable when surface, edge treatment, core and cleaning conditions are specified for the project.

What is a practical bathroom door for the Philippines?

WPC is a common project option for wet areas, but final construction, frame and hardware should be checked against the opening and maintenance plan.

Can deliveries be phased across a construction schedule?

Yes. Release quantities by handover phase and freeze each shipment’s lot, packing list and installation information.

How many doors are in a 40HQ for a condominium project?

The count depends on configuration, size mix, packaging and weight; confirm with a project-specific loading plan.
