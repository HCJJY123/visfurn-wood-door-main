Private-Label White Primed Flush Doors for an Italian Distributor: EN 14351-2, Metric Sizes, Own Brand

An Italian building products distributor built its own door range in China: 800x2000mm metric sizes, adjustable jambs, CE documentation and neutral private-label packaging.

Direct Answer

The representative Italian distribution package used metric white primed flush door sets, telescopic adjustable jambs and neutral retail-ready packaging so the distributor could build a consistent private-label range.

The challenge

The distributor wanted to build a private-label range, but fixed jambs created wall-thickness mismatch and return risk. Retail presentation also required neutral, brand-ready packaging.

The specification

The representative range used 700, 800 and 900 × 2000mm metric door sets, 40mm leaves, telescopic adjustable jambs covering 80–140mm and a matte white primed finish suitable for a later site coat when correctly prepared.

What changed

The range moved to telescopic jambs, the cartons were made neutral with a reserved brand area, and the documentation package was organised so downstream customers could request the relevant DoP and test files.

Logistics and results

The representative route ran from Xingang toward Genoa or La Spezia in approximately 30–40 days, subject to routing. The anonymised project summary records a reduction in jamb-mismatch returns and a repeat-order framework, not a universal performance guarantee.

FAQ

Questions buyers usually ask

Do internal doors need CE marking in Italy?

Confirm the applicable product scope, harmonised standard and documentation route with the responsible manufacturer and Italian project consultant.

What is an adjustable jamb range?

It is the defined finished-wall range that a telescopic jamb can accommodate; confirm the actual range and trim profile before production.

Can a Chinese factory do private-label packaging?

Yes, neutral cartons and agreed brand areas can be reviewed, but artwork, marks and claims should be approved before mass production.

What is the standard European door size?

800 × 2000 mm is a common reference, but the project schedule and local system determine the final sizes.

Who issues the Declaration of Performance?

The responsible manufacturer or legally defined economic operator issues the relevant document when the product falls within the applicable regulatory scope.
