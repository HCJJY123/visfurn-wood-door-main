Guestroom door packages built around room schedules and corridor consistency.

Hotel guestroom doors are specified differently from ordinary interior doors. They face the public corridor, carry heavier hardware, affect acoustic comfort and must remain visually consistent across many openings. Visfurn reviews the leaf, frame, architrave, seal, electronic lock preparation and finish batch as one project package.

The door set can be prepared for electronic locks, viewers, indicators, closers and hinges before finishing. This reduces site cutting, protects finish quality and helps room-by-room installation follow the project schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is acoustic sealing important for guestroom doors?

Corridor noise often leaks through the door perimeter. A denser leaf only works when paired with compression seals and a bottom seal, so the door should be quoted as a sealed set.

Can locks and closers be prepared before finishing?

Yes. Send the lock model and closer drawing so mortices, bores and reinforcement can be machined cleanly before finish application.

How do you control finish consistency?

Finish material is reserved from one batch where possible, control samples are retained and doors are produced based on the room schedule.

How should buyers send the enquiry?

Send room schedule, opening size, wall thickness, frame type, hardware models, finish reference, acoustic or fire-rated requirement, quantity and destination.
