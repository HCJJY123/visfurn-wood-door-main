Hospital Door Buying FAQ

What details are required for hospital doors?

Send the room schedule, opening size, wall thickness, movement flow, vision panel need, hardware list and cleaning or hygiene requirements so the hospital door package can be reviewed properly.

Can hospital doors support clean rooms or ICU areas?

Yes. Hermetic sliding or controlled swing door options can be reviewed when pressure, sealing and access requirements are defined in the project specification.

What should be included in the quotation request?

Include drawings, quantities, frame depth, finish direction, hardware models and destination port to obtain a useful project quotation.

What should a hospital door RFQ include?

Separate patient rooms, wards, utility, clean and imaging openings, then provide opening size, wall thickness, frame, hygiene surface, vision panel, impact protection, hardware, access flow and any shielding requirement.

Hospital Door Specification Questions

How should hospital doors be separated by room type?

Separate patient rooms, wards, operating support areas, imaging rooms, clean corridors and utility rooms. Each zone may need different hygiene surface, frame, vision panel, impact protection or shielding details.

When is a lead-lined door required?

Lead-lined doors should be reviewed only when the project drawings or radiation shielding consultant specifies lead equivalence, wall shielding and frame protection. The door, frame and glass must be coordinated as a system.

What makes a hospital door easier to maintain?

Smooth surfaces, sealed edges, impact plates, moisture-resistant frames and replaceable hardware reduce maintenance pressure in high-traffic healthcare corridors.

What should be included in a hospital RFQ?

Send the room schedule, hygiene requirement, opening size, wall thickness, vision panel, hardware set, impact protection, shielding requirement and destination port.
