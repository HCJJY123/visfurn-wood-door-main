Project Buying FAQ

What projects are white moulded interior doors suitable for?

They are suitable for apartments, hotels, villas, offices and other interior projects that need a clean white primed door with stable engineered construction.

What core options can be specified?

Available core options include honeycomb, tubular and solid mineral core structures. The final choice depends on budget, durability, acoustic targets and project specification.

Can the size and finish be customized?

Yes. Standard sizes include 80 inch, 84 inch, 2100 mm and 2400 mm heights with common interior widths, and project-specific dimensions or factory lacquer finishes can be quoted.

What is the MOQ for this door?

The listed MOQ is 50 pieces. Larger project quantities can use tiered pricing and opening-specific labeling for easier site installation.

How are moulded interior doors packed for export?

Doors are packed with EPE foam, reinforced cardboard corner protectors, export pallets and opening-specific labels to reduce transit damage and simplify distribution on site.
