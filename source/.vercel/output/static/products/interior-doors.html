Buyer Answer

Which interior doors should B2B buyers specify first?

Start with the room schedule: apartment bedrooms and living areas usually use painted or engineered interior doors, while hotel guestroom entries may need acoustic cores, closer preparation and electronic lock drawings. Bathrooms and utility rooms should be reviewed separately for moisture exposure.

Prepare for quotation

  • Door leaf size, wall thickness and opening quantity
  • Frame, architrave, finish sample and color reference
  • Lock, hinge, closer and handing requirements
  • Destination port, packing marks and project timeline
  • Apartment interior door selection guide

Quick answer

An interior-door package becomes quote-ready when the schedule defines the leaf, frame, jamb depth, finish, hardware preparation and packing sequence for each repeated opening.

For apartments, hotels and mixed-use projects, a nominal door size alone does not control the full set. A schedule that includes finished wall thickness, handing and the supply scope prevents missing frames, architraves or prepared hardware at installation.

Interior Door Buying FAQ

Which interior doors are suitable for apartment and hotel projects?

White moulded doors, engineered wooden doors, acoustic entry doors and WPC doors can be reviewed according to room type, traffic level, acoustic needs and moisture exposure.

What should buyers prepare before requesting a quote?

Prepare the opening schedule, leaf size, wall thickness, frame profile, finish reference, hardware drawings, quantity and destination.

Can interior doors be customized?

Yes. Size, finish, frame, architrave and hardware preparation can be reviewed before production according to project drawings and approved samples.

Should buyers send rough opening or finished door leaf size?

For bulk projects, send both if available. Rough opening helps confirm frame allowance, while leaf size, wall thickness and jamb depth help VISFURN quote the correct prehung or full door set.

How can a door schedule reduce quotation errors?

A room-by-room door schedule connects opening ID, quantity, handing, finish, frame, architrave and hardware prep, reducing missing parts during container loading and site installation.
