Where Acoustic Doors Actually Fail

An acoustic rating is a property of an assembly, not of a panel. A leaf tested at Rw 32 dB and then hung in an unsealed frame with a 10 mm gap under it will measure somewhere in the low twenties on site, because sound follows air. That gap between the lab number and the handover measurement is what generates complaints on apartment projects, and it is almost always a sealing problem rather than a door problem.

This door is built and quoted as a complete opening. The leaf uses a laminated plywood stile-and-rail frame — the plywood carries the load and holds the entry lock and closer screws, while the density-graded infill between the rails supplies the mass that blocks airborne sound. Around the perimeter, a three-sided compression gasket sits in a routed groove and a drop-down bottom seal closes the threshold. Hardware positions are machined before finishing, so nobody is cutting into a sealed edge on site.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much noise reduction does a soundproof composite entry door actually give?

The tested leaf and frame assembly is rated at Rw 32 dB nominal, which brings normal corridor conversation and lift-lobby noise down to a level most residents stop noticing. The number only holds if the door is hung with its own frame and the full perimeter seal fitted; a rated leaf installed in an unsealed frame typically loses eight to twelve decibels of that performance, which is the single most common reason an acoustic door underperforms on site.

Why use a plywood composite core instead of a hollow or honeycomb core?

Acoustic performance depends on mass and on breaking up the resonance of the panel. A hollow core has neither, so it behaves like a drum. The laminated plywood stile-and-rail frame carries the structural load and the hardware, while the density-graded infill between the rails adds the mass that blocks airborne sound. It also gives far better screw-holding for entry locks and heavy closers than a honeycomb board can.

Is this door suitable for an exterior opening?

It is designed as an entrance door for a protected opening — an apartment unit door off an internal corridor, a hotel guestroom door or an office suite entrance. It is not a weather-exposed front door. For an opening that takes direct rain, sun or wind-driven moisture, specify a WPC or fibreglass exterior door instead and we will quote that separately.

Can locks, closers and hinges be prepared at the factory?

Yes. Lock bores, hinge mortices, viewer holes and closer reinforcement plates are machined to your hardware drawing before finishing. Entry doors carry heavier ironmongery than interior doors, so factory reinforcement in the correct positions matters more here. Sending the hardware schedule with your enquiry lets us confirm the reinforcement layout before production.

What sizes and finishes are available?

Standard leaf sizes are 900×2100 mm and 36"×80", with width, height and thickness cut to your opening schedule. Finishes cover PVC film, melamine and PU-painted veneer in woodgrain and solid colours, with the frame and architrave laminated from the same batch so colour matches across the opening.

How are the doors packed and how many fit a container?

Each leaf is corner-protected, foam-wrapped and shrink-filmed, then stacked on export pallets with the frames packed as separate bundles keyed to the same opening reference. A 40HQ container carries roughly 180–220 leaves at 45 mm, and the exact figure depends on whether frames ship in the same container.
